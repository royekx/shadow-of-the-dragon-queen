Journey011 The Moons Over Bracken Hollow Open Record
A list from Wyhan draws the Embers to Bracken Hollow, a village under three eclipsed moons. No one has been able to leave it for a month. Sergeant Boyd explains the turn of the white, red and black moons, and the eclipse that takes one villager each time. The party climbs the hill with Corvin and Ked and brings down the stone monument by darkening the three rings on its chest, but the Emberwake's fury takes Aratos and Roshi has to drop him. Beneath the hill are three recesses, and three things to find in the village before the next eclipse.

Journey010 The Weight of the Axe Open Record
Fizban's stew recipe turns out to be a cipher for a spell. Finbar takes Jasper to meet the head of the Dragon's Eye in Kalaman. In a sparring match the trophy axe's rage overtakes Aratos and he nearly kills a squire. Marshal Vendri sends the party, recorded as the Embers, to find the tinkerer Tatina Rookledust. On the way out, at the reliquary, Aratos holds the axe's fire back through resolve.

Journey007 Flames Over Vogler Open Record
A dragon burns the docks as the last boats load. Its rider, Theros Flametongue, duels Aratos. Jasper sees that the dragon is a crewed machine. Mayariel kills Theros, and Aratos brings the machine down with his javelin of lightning. From the boat the party watches Becklin fall into the river and Theros rise again.

Journey002 The Road After Bloodshed Open Record
The party buries the fallen knights, burns the scaled dead and takes what clues the knights carried. In the night Jasper dreams of his guild, a golden canary visits Mayariel, and Kakashi sees the moons merge. On the road they pull an old man out of a creature of living sand. He is Fizban, a green-robed wizard and friend of Ispin. He freezes and shatters the creature, then rides with them to Vogler.

The one they capture was sent by a Shadow Queen, says the east has fallen, and kills itself.
