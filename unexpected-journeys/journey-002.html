After the ambush, the road grew still. Roshi dug graves for the fallen knights and treated the dead with solemn care. Aratos recognized the armor of the Knights of Solamnia, the order he had set out to find, and on one body he found a written briefing: the knights had been sent to look into attacks on local farmers. The others gathered weapons, coin, and two healing potions. The bodies of the reptilian attackers decayed with disturbing speed, and the party chose fire rather than leaving them to rot beside the road.

Night brought no true peace. On the first watch Roshi thought of Ispin, the human adventurer she had traveled with and visited in Vogler every few years. Jasper's sleep returned him to the shadow of the Dragon's Eye, the guild that had marked him and bound him with a deadly coin. In the dream the guildmaster stood behind him again, a man with a violet scarf whom Jasper had once conned with a cursed ring. Jasper had been given an impossible task: join the mages, become one of them, and so reach the moving Tower of Wayreth, which holds the Orb of Dragonkind. If he ran, the guild would find him. If he failed, the mark on his hand would kill him. The words of the guild clung to him like a curse: none shall hide from the Dragon's Eye. Jasper woke with his heart racing and kept the dream to himself.

During the second watch a small golden canary landed on Mayariel's shoulder, greeted her briefly, and flew away. Kakashi took the last watch alone, and it brought its own omen. He saw the three moons draw together and merge into one impossible shape. Three voices told him he was not strong enough, not yet, and that he needed more. Images flooded his mind of archmages bending the elements to their will, summoning titanic beasts, and shaping time itself. His eyes flared with power, and the hunger for greater magic stirred in him with new urgency.

The next day, with the stranded travelers aboard their caravan, the road itself seemed to betray them. One of the elves spotted a human foot sticking out of the road. Jasper approached with caution and said hello, and the foot began to kick. Then the ground started to swallow Jasper's feet as well. As the party fought to free him, a living mass of sand rose around them.

The battle against the sand creature was desperate and strange. Jasper's spear tore an opening and showed the face of a very old man, upside down inside the sand, who treated his predicament with baffling cheer. Well met, he said, and he asked to be pulled out so that he could help. Then Roshi was struck hard and nearly killed. With help from Kakashi's raven and an offered spear, the old man hauled himself out. Mayariel rushed to preserve Roshi's life. Kakashi recognized the old man's incantation as a cone of cold, poured a healing potion into Roshi, and dragged her clear. Once everyone was out of the way, the old man loosed a cone of frost from his staff that froze the creature solid. Jasper's next blow cracked it, Aratos hurled his javelin through it in a streak of lightning, and the old man shattered it with a burst of air. When the danger passed, he introduced himself as Fizban, a peculiar wizard in patchwork green robes and another old friend of Ispin. He had been on his way to Vogler to say his goodbyes.

Fizban joined them in the caravan for the road to Vogler. Roshi turned her divine sense on him and saw a mortal man with a faint golden aura. He took a particular interest in Mayariel's divine magic, noting that clerics had not been seen for some three hundred years and that if the gods were giving people magic again, there had to be a cause. She named the Platinum Dragon as her god, and he approved. When he learned there was a wizard in training aboard, he spoke plainly about the Test of High Sorcery: it was terrifying and could be cutthroat, and it was best to learn as much as possible first. He asked whether Wyhan in Kalaman was the one to see. Wyhan was rough around the edges and very powerful, he said, and courtesy would serve them well. He was comic and unsettlingly insightful at once.

At last, the party reached Vogler. The village stood beside the Vingaard River, preparing for a festival, and Ispin's celebration of life was to be held at the Brass Crab. Roshi went off alone toward the Fish Bowl. Mayariel walked down to the docks. Jasper explored and learned of Thornwall Keep. Alone in the market square, he noticed a hunched old man with a strange shimmer about him. Inside the old man's shape Jasper saw a much smaller figure, and it gave him a knowing look. The town itself seemed warm and full of ordinary life.
