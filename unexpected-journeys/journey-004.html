Tales, Tears, and Treachery

Ispin's memory became a shield, and Vogler became a battlefield.

As sunset came to Vogler, the town gathered by the river to say farewell to Ispin Greenshield. His body lay wrapped in a shroud inside a small boat on the dock. Becklin stood before the mourners in her Solamnic armor and spoke with gentle strength. Ispin had asked for tales instead of tears, and so the town gave him what he wanted. Villagers carried the boat to the dock's edge, where Becklin and Cudgel lowered it into the Vingaard River. As it drifted into the fading light, the people watched until the vessel sank from sight.

The stillness of the river gave way to the roar of the Brass Crab. Inside, grief became laughter, tankards clashed, food was passed freely, and stories of Ispin rose from every corner of the room. Some swore he had ridden down a volcano on his shield. Others claimed he had leapt from a mountain into shallow water and survived. Becklin remembered a so-called little adventure against a wyrmling that ended with her and Ispin laughing their way out of disaster. Fizban told a tale of a stolen pie and an offended prince.

Not every voice honored the dead kindly. Bakaris the Younger, a blond young nobleman with more arrogance than grace, kept interrupting to say the stories were being told wrong. For the moment the room let it pass.

Roshi added a story of Ispin and a bear den, telling how he had once gone after honey and waited for the bears to wake so he could compliment them on it. The tale ended with Roshi wrestling the mother bear to save him, and the absurdity of it fit perfectly among the other impossible memories. Then Mayariel spoke more solemnly. She admitted she had only known of Ispin and had never known the man. She described him as someone guided by curiosity rather than fear, who treated life as an invitation to explore, ask questions, change, and find joy in the unknown. As she spoke, the room fell silent. When she finished, the people raised their drinks to Ispin, and Becklin told her it was very well said.

The night also gave the party a clearer shape. Becklin introduced herself properly, and the travelers gave their names. Kakashi said he had never met Ispin and had only heard the tales. Bakaris called out that none of the tales were true, and Becklin's patience ended. She told him that if he had nothing fond to share, he should retire for the evening. He protested that it was a joke, said he and his father had important matters to attend to, and walked out. Becklin excused him to the party as a brat.

The question of what came next hung over them. Fizban said he had to leave for Kalaman in the morning on pressing business. He added that Mayariel's vision worried him, and that he meant to see whether he could arrange an audience for them in the city. Aratos pressed the need to warn the Knights of Solamnia and asked Mayariel whether she would come. Mayariel weighed her own vision and the danger it implied, and agreed to travel with them for a while. They had fought together and mourned together, and they were beginning to choose the same road.

Becklin then remembered that Ispin had wanted his green shield to go to Fizban. Fizban declined it. He said the shield needed someone who would stand on the front line, and he offered it to Ispin's friends at the table. Roshi asked for it, and Kakashi agreed that it belonged with her. Becklin had left the shield at Thornwall Keep and said her squire, Darrett, would fetch them in the morning. She asked Mayor Raven to house the group for the night, and the mayor welcomed them to her home.

Jasper's night took a secret turn. He found a note in his pocket written in Thieves' Cant, telling him to meet at General Goods and come alone. He told the others only that he had a meeting. Aratos meant to follow him, but Jasper overheard the whisper, waited, and slipped out unseen.

With Jasper gone, Aratos told Becklin his vision. He had stood alone on a ridge while a swarm of creatures charged with weapons drawn. He said that Mayariel had seen something similar, and the others described the reptilian soldiers from the road and the words one of them had spoken: the Shadow Queen approaches. Becklin knew of no such creatures, but she believed their concern was genuine. She could not leave her post, so she promised to send them with a letter that might help them gain an audience in Kalaman. She warned that a retired knight's word might carry less than she would like. She had heard nothing from Kalaman in some time, and the last people to come from the city had been Lord Bakaris and his son.

Jasper went alone to General Goods and circled to the yard behind it. There a blade came to his throat and a voice gave him half a phrase: none shall hide. Jasper did not know the answer. The old man behind the blade shimmered, and Jasper's coin showed him a small figure inside the old man's shape, another member of the Dragon's Eye. Old Man Jenkins had feared that Lucky had sent Jasper to kill him. Once that was settled he taught him the rest of the phrase, none shall hide from the Dragon's Eye, and warned that guild members elsewhere would cut his throat for not knowing it. Lucky was the guild master and not to be crossed, and the coin would kill Jasper if he did not do as he was told. When Jasper spoke of stealing the Orb of Dragonkind from the moving tower, Jenkins called the job insane and said he might as well put rocks on his back and walk into the river. The Tower of Wayreth never stays in one place, and only the wizards know how to reach it. If Jasper needed more, there were others like them in Kalaman. He also sold Jasper a pair of winged boots, which Jasper paid for from the party's shared gold.

The others went to bed at Mayor Raven's house without him. Past midnight Aratos went outside to wait. Jasper came up the path, showed off the boots, and called them a slick deal. He said only that he knew a guy and had drawn on the shared purse. He asked whether the meeting had gone well. Jasper said it had, and told him nothing of the guild.

Morning brought parting gifts and new burdens. Kakashi found a note from Fizban on his nightstand. It apologized for the hasty departure, said that Fizban had taken Aeimos along, and asked Kakashi to let Aratos know. With it was a parting gift, the spell Shield. Mayariel woke to a golden canary hopping on her nightstand beside a pendant. When she reached for the pendant, the bird stopped chirping and a voice spoke through it to her alone. It said the pendant had served clerics before her and might aid her divine path, and it hoped to help her more when they met again. Mayariel mentioned to the others only that she had an amulet too. The party then divided its gear. Kakashi took the winged boots, Jasper took the boots of false tracks, and Roshi took the Moon-Touched Sword.

Darrett, Becklin's young squire, arrived to bring the party to Thornwall Keep. There, Becklin gave Roshi Ispin's Green Shield. When Roshi took it, it felt as though the shield had come home, and she learned that it was magical, which she had never known. The gift came with one request: its new bearer should fight in the festival's mock battle, which Ispin had loved. Becklin still owed them the letter and promised to write it. Asked about other wizards, she said the nearest thing Vogler had was Than, who lodged with her and was more of a tinkerer.

The party agreed to take part in the Kingfisher Festival's mock battle, a reenactment of Vogler's founding conflict. Before the event, Aratos wanted to question Lord Bakaris about Kalaman, and Darrett walked them to the Bakaris house. The father was out. Bakaris the Younger was leaning on a fence, just as insufferable as before. He said that he and his father came from Kalaman and had connections there. Aratos and Kakashi tried to draw more from him, but it became clear the young noble had very little true authority. Aratos failed to cow him. Kakashi struck his staff on the ground and learned that Lord Bakaris would be judging the mock battle. Afterward Roshi studied the shield and found she could wrap herself in the illusion of a boulder or a tree. Darrett sparred with her so she could try it, and Aratos told him to trust himself when he led the charge.

At High Hill, the festival reached its martial heart. Mayor Raven led the parade, and Lord Bakaris stood among the spectators. Darrett stood at the center of the militia, and Cudgel's mercenaries took the role of the old Istarian foe. At first, it was all pageantry. Darrett called the charge, Aratos moved to support him, Roshi strode into the fray, Mayariel joined the fighting with awkward enthusiasm, Jasper moved freely through the chaos, and Kakashi took to the air in the winged boots. Then a mercenary's unpadded blade slashed into one of the militia, and the screaming started.

The mock battle became a massacre, and the traitors went for Darrett first. Jasper reacted instantly, killing a mercenary before Darrett could fall. Aratos shouted to his companions to protect the militia and kept Darrett close. Roshi stood against the attackers with Ispin's shield and moon-touched steel. Mayariel's divine magic flashed, sometimes wild, sometimes devastating. Kakashi struggled to fight from the air but kept pressing his magic into the battle. Aratos called for one attacker to be taken alive, and in the press one mercenary was struck on the helmet and knocked senseless. Then Roshi saw more enemies riding in, and with them came a huge half-ogre.

The half-ogre nearly crushed Roshi, driving her close to collapse, but she answered with divine fire. Her blade erupted with searing power and tore into the creature, its bluish-purple blood splattering across her as she refused to give ground. Around her, the others cut down attackers one by one. Aratos felled one of the newcomers with his battle axe. Jasper struck from cover with blade and bow. Darrett found his courage and killed a mercenary himself. The half-ogre drank a healing potion in the middle of the fight, and Mayariel's light burned into it again.

At last, Jasper shot the half-ogre through the head, and Roshi threw herself clear on Ispin's shield as the massive body fell. Becklin and Cudgel entered the field, cutting through the remaining attackers and pushing the ambush into retreat. Cudgel was horrified to find her own mercenaries had betrayed her, and swore to get to the bottom of it. Kakashi searched the half-ogre and found a potion, some gold, and a note: "For your services from the Red Army." He shared it with his companions.

Many of the militia were dead or badly hurt. Becklin set Darrett to getting the wounded off the field. The party offered to help find out who had ordered the attack and why the mercenaries had turned, and was told that all help was welcome. The captured mercenary, still unconscious, was locked in Vogler's small jail for questioning. Back in town, Mayor Raven was shaken and set on seeing it answered, and the party rested and began to plan.
