The Knights Radiant
Ancient knightly order

A lost order tied to hidden reliquaries. Its symbol is a sword, and its doors ask for oaths: Valor, Resolve, Foresight. Aratos and Aeimos have been named children of Cathan by one of its dead. Its tombs and legacies are being targeted by Lord Soth.
