The Red Dragon Army
Enemy army

An invading army serving the Dragon Queen, organized and well supplied. It fields armored soldiers, draconians and a war machine built like a dragon, and it paid mercenaries to turn on Vogler. It burned Vogler and is pressing other villages too.
