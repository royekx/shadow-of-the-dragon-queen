The Dragon's Eye
Thieves' guild

A secretive thieves' guild that marks and binds its members with a coin. Its phrase is: none shall hide from the Dragon's Eye. Its guild master is Lucky Leppomanto. It keeps a hidden branch in Kalaman behind the sign of Peller's Provisions.
