War Intel

The powers shaping the war, as far as the Embers know them
The Enemy Who is marching, and in whose name.
D
The Dragon Queen
The queen the enemy serves. Her soldiers call her the Shadow Queen and the Dragon Queen and say they are reclaiming the realm for her. Mayriel has seen her in vision as a horned woman in dark regalia who unfolds into a five-headed dragon.
R
The Red Dragon Army
An invading army serving the Dragon Queen, organized and well supplied. It fields armored soldiers, draconians and a war machine built like a dragon, and it paid mercenaries to turn on Vogler. It burned Vogler and is pressing other villages too.
D
Draconians
Reptilian humanoids with scaled hides, sharp claws and brutal weapons. They speak Draconic and fight in coordination. A captured one killed itself sooner than talk. Their bodies decay with unnatural speed, and a red one exploded when it was killed.
Orders Knights and wizards, living and lost.
K
The Knights of Solamnia
The knighthood Aratos set out to reach and warn of what his vision showed. Becklin Uth Viharin was a retired knight of the order. Lord Soth was once one of its most celebrated knights and fell into disgrace. His statue still stands in Kalaman's courtyard.
K
The Knights Radiant
An order tied to hidden reliquaries. Its symbol is a sword. The door of its reliquary outside Kalaman asked for oaths and answered with the virtues of knighthood: Valor, Resolve, Foresight. Aratos and Aeimos have been named children of Cathan by one of its dead. Its tombs and legacies are being targeted by Lord Soth.
M
The Mages of High Sorcery
An order of wizards. Kakashi and Jasper carry its scrolls and are to test for its White Robes. Its towers are built to keep powerful magical things safe from those who would misuse them. In Kalaman its business runs through Wyhan, an apothecary.
Guilds Those who work out of sight.
D
The Dragon's Eye
A secretive thieves' guild that marks and binds its members with a coin. Its phrase is: none shall hide from the Dragon's Eye. Its guild master is Lucky Leppomanto. It keeps a hidden branch in Kalaman behind the sign of Peller's Provisions.
