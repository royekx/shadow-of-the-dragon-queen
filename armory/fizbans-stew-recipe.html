Fizban's Stew Recipe
Ispin's campfire stew, in Fizban's hand

A scrap of parchment recording Ispin Greenshield's campfire stew, each ingredient in an absurd quantity. The numbers were a cipher pointing to words in the recipe itself, and the words were an incantation. Spoken aloud, it taught all five of the party the Green Flame Blade and burned to nothing.

Journey 010
  • Fell from where Fizban had been sitting; Roshi picked it up
  • Its reminder: do not forget the fire, the blade, or the green bits
  • Kakashi suspected the quantities were not measurements
  • Wyhan, given only the numbers, judged that they decipher part of the recipe into an incantation
  • Roshi worked out the words and spoke them: blade made bright, let emerald fire arise, strike one foe, then leap before their eyes
  • Green flame touched each of the five and the parchment burned away
