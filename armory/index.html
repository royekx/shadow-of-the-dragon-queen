A Amulet of PaladineA gift that came with the canary Wondrous Item Mayariel

The Amulet of Paladine is a silver pendant Mayariel woke to in Vogler alongside a golden canary. A voice speaking through the bird said clerics had used it before her, that some call it the Amulet of the Faithful, and that it might aid her divine path.

A Armor of the FallenThe necromancer's armor Armor Kakashi

An intricate armor of bone, heavy with necrotic energy, taken from the body of the necromancer who served Lord Soth. Kakashi wears it. It fitted itself to him and bound to his soul, and if he dies it is lost.

B Becklin's ArmorPlate left for Darrett Armor Darrett Highwater

Plate armor that Becklin Uth Viharin left for Darrett in a chest at Thornwall Keep. Darrett would not take it at first. He wears it now as a promise to grow into the burden.

B The Black SeedA knot of black from under Gribb Brack's field Gift Seated beneath the hill

A jet-black knot the size of a stone, shaped like a seed, that would look rotten anywhere else. Jasper watched an echo of a younger Gribb Brack bury it in a corner of the field nobody worked. Roots that pulsed with light ran from it, and Kakashi read their magic as drawing on life. When it was lifted, two black wolves rose out of the ground. It belonged to the black moon. Tien seated it beneath the hill, last of the three, and the spell broke.

B Boilerdrake HeartFire gem from the war machine Gem Kakashi

A hot, glowing gem pulled from the wreckage of the mechanical dragon that burned Vogler. It was the source of the machine's fire.

B Boots of False TracksBoots that disguise their wearer's trail Wondrous Item Jasper

A pair of boots found unworn in the cavern beneath Crustdurn Isle. Whoever wears them can make their tracks look like something else's to anyone trying to follow. Jasper took them when the party divided its gear.

D Dragon VesselA vessel that serves as a potion in reserve Wondrous Item Jasper

A vessel that can be used as a potion, a drinking horn or any other container. The party counts on it as a healing draught in reserve. Jasper carries it.

D The Dragon's Eye CoinMark of the guild Wondrous Item Jasper

The golden coin, set into Jasper's hand, by which the Dragon's Eye marked and bound him. It will kill him if he does not do as the guild master says. It lets him see through the disguises of other guild members, and it can lend him another face.

E The Emberwake GreataxeCursed trophy axe Weapon Aratos

A great axe that runs warm to the touch. Aratos took it from Theros Flametongue's body as Vogler burned. It is cursed. A hit in a sparring match woke a rage in him that was not his own, and it took him again on the hill at Bracken Hollow. He cannot hand it to anyone or part with it, and other weapons now sit wrong in his hand. It went cold once, at the reliquary, when he chose resolve, and for a moment it became the Leviathan Axe. On the hill at Bracken Hollow it forced itself into his hand, and for the first time there was pleasure in the killing. He let the rage go when he felt it turning on his friends.

F Fizban's Stew RecipeIspin's campfire stew, in Fizban's hand Document Burned away

A scrap of parchment recording Ispin Greenshield's campfire stew, each ingredient in an absurd quantity. The numbers were a cipher pointing to words in the recipe itself, and the words were an incantation. Spoken aloud, it taught all five of the party the Green Flame Blade and burned to nothing.

H The Horn of Sir CthondorHorn from Sir Cthondor's statue Wondrous Item Aratos

The horn from the statue of Sir Cthondor, at the center of the Radiant Reliquary. Blown, it makes no sound a mortal can hear. Spectral warriors answer it: two knights in the tomb, and five on the hill at Bracken Hollow. It answers once in seven days, and the warriors stay an hour.

I Ispin's Green ShieldShield of a legend Shield Roshi

The green shield Ispin Greenshield carried through a life of impossible stories. He left it to Fizban, who declined it and offered it to Ispin's friends. When Roshi took it, it felt as though the shield had come home. On the hill at Bracken Hollow, as she remembered standing in front of Ispin at the bear den, vines grew from it and held her where she stood, and she heard his chuckle. Now Fizban is asking for it.

J Javelin of LightningA javelin that strikes as a bolt of lightning Weapon Aratos

Aratos's javelin. Thrown with its command word, it becomes a streak of lightning that passes through whatever stands in its path. It brought down the dragon machine over Vogler.

L Leeching ArrowsArrows that draw life from their target Ammunition Jasper

Arrows with dried leeches fixed to the heads, meant to latch on and draw life from whatever they strike. Nobody had wanted to buy them. Jenkins handed them over, grudgingly, when Jasper chose to stay and defend Vogler.

L Longbow of WarningA longbow that hums when danger is near Weapon Mayariel

Mayariel's longbow. It hums when danger is close, and it has done so before an ambush on the road and before the wolves at Bracken Hollow.

M Mayariel's Signet RingFamily signet, pledged to Paladine Ring Mayariel

Mayariel's family signet ring. On the burning docks of Vogler she offered it to Paladine, with a promise of obedience when the god one day asks something of her. It took on a faint divine glow and stayed on her hand.

M Moon-Marked SpellbookReward of the mage trial Wondrous Item Kakashi

A spellbook marked with the moons, handed over by a red-robed proctor when Kakashi and Jasper passed the entry exam of the Mages of High Sorcery.

M Moon-Touched SwordBlade from beneath the isle Weapon Roshi

A moon-touched sword found in the cavern beneath Crustdurn Isle after the beast there was killed.

P Periapt of Wound ClosureAmulet that holds off death Wondrous Item Roshi

A magical amulet found on one of the Red Army dead at Vogler, on the same body as the blue-gemmed ring. Identified as a Periapt of Wound Closure, which preserves its wearer from death when they fall.

R The Red BannerThe banner over Bracken Hollow's gate Gift Seated beneath the hill

A grain sack with the village's mark painted on it. Becklin brought it to Boyd in the Blood Summer, twenty-seven years ago, when hobgoblins besieged Bracken Hollow, and it was flown over the gate. After the fight that followed the hobgoblins never came back. When Jasper took it down, a hobgoblin captain formed out of red wisps behind him, and the eclipse came early. It belonged to the red moon. Jumanji seated it beneath the hill.

R Ring of ProtectionA blue-gemmed ring of protective magic Ring Kakashi

A blue-gemmed ring found on one of the Red Army dead at Vogler, on the same body as the periapt. Identified as a Ring of Protection. Kakashi wears it.

S Spear of WarningJasper's spear, a weapon of warning Weapon Jasper

Jasper's spear, a weapon of warning like Mayariel's longbow. He threw it at the monument at Bracken Hollow and missed, and it still lies on the hillside.

S Storm-Charged GauntletsAeimos's gauntlets Weapon Aeimos

The gauntlets Aeimos fights with. Activated, they strike with lightning, even from a distance.

W The White ToothA crescent of bone on a cord Gift Seated beneath the hill

A small crescent of ivory on a cord, a gnarled and curving tooth, most likely a hobgoblin's tusk. Tien felt holy, healing power in it. Marigold gave it to Miren when his wife Sela was dying of the Pale Fever, and when he put it on her she recovered. She wore it until she gave it to Tien, and then she died. The tusk of the hobgoblin captain at the gate was the same shape. It belonged to the white moon. Mayariel seated it beneath the hill.
