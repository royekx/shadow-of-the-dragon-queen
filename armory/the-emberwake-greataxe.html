The Emberwake Greataxe
Cursed trophy axe

A great axe that runs warm constantly, like an ember that never dies. Aratos took it from Theros Flametongue's body as Vogler burned. It is cursed: a real blow against its bearer can wake a rage that is not his own, he cannot hand it away or set it down, and other weapons now sit wrong in his hand. Restraint, not fury, is what quiets it.
