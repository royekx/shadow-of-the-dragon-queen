Theros Flametongue
Orcish warrior with a flaming axe

Theros Flametongue is a green-skinned orcish warrior who rode into Vogler on the dragon machine carrying a flaming axe. Mayriel killed him on the docks. As the boats pulled away, a figure stood over his body, flame passed across him, and he rose.
