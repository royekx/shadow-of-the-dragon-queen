Darrett Highwater
Becklin's former squire

Darrett Highwater was Becklin's young squire in Vogler. He survived the town's fall, accepted her armor as a promise to grow into it, and now stands as the Embers' proxy with Kalaman so they are not formally conscripted. He trains at the castle grounds and sleeps among the Voglerans.
