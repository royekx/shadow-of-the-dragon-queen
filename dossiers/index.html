B Bakaris the ElderLord; nobleman Neutral Journey 008

Lord Bakaris is a nobleman who dresses in red and gold and takes offense when plans are made without him. He prefers a sealed letter to a ride into danger. He reached Kalaman's council ahead of the refugees.

B Bakaris the YoungerYoung nobleman Neutral Journey 008

Bakaris the Younger is a blond young nobleman, son of Lord Bakaris, with more arrogance than grace and very little real authority.

Becklin Uth ViharinRetired Knight of Solamnia Ally Journey 007

Becklin Uth Viharin was a retired Knight of Solamnia living in Vogler and an old friend of Ispin Greenshield. She believed the party's warnings, gave Roshi Ispin's shield, and held the docks so the boats could leave. She took two slashes across her back and fell into the Vingaard.

C The Charred SwordswomanUnidentified warrior Adversary Journey 007

A tall woman with charred skin, blackened armor and a massive sword, seen through the smoke at Vogler's waterfront. She called after the party's boat that they would not escape the flames of the Dragon Queen. The party does not know her name.

C CorvinVillager of Bracken Hollow Unknown Journey 011

Corvin is a broad-shouldered man in his forties with a rough beard, one of those who go out under the black moon for food. He told the party what Boyd would not: that another of the black-touched can be sent up the hill in place of the one the eclipse chooses. He believes the village must make the best of that. On the hill he kept Ked from leaving at the point of a dagger.

C Sir CthondorKnight of the hidden reliquary, at rest Ally Journey 010

Sir Cthondor was a knight whose tomb lies in a hidden reliquary of the Knights Radiant outside Kalaman. A necromancer serving Lord Soth raised his spirit as a wraith. Freed, he named Aratos and Aeimos children of Cathan and sounded his horn to send the risen dead back to rest.

C Cudgel IronsmileMercenary captain Ally Journey 007

Cudgel Ironsmile is a mercenary captain whose company played the old foe in Vogler's festival battle. Part of that company sold her out to the Red Army. She fought for the town to the end and was last seen kicked through a building on the burning waterfront.

D Darrett HighwaterBecklin's former squire Ally Journey 010

Darrett Highwater was Becklin's young squire in Vogler. He survived the town's fall, accepted her armor as a promise to grow into it, and now stands as the Embers' proxy with Kalaman so they are not formally conscripted. He trains at the castle grounds and sleeps among the Voglerans.

F FinbarDragon's Eye contact Neutral Journey 010

Finbar is a kender of the Dragon's Eye who can pass unseen. In Vogler he wore the face of Old Man Jenkins and kept General Goods. Jasper's coin showed him the figure inside the disguise, and before Vogler fell he had the name as well. The rest of the party still knows him only as Jenkins. In Kalaman he walked Jasper to the guild behind a different human face, and showed his own only inside its door.

FizbanGreen-robed wizard, old friend of Ispin Ally Journey 010

Fizban is a peculiar old wizard in green robes, found trapped inside a living sand creature on the road to Vogler. He comes and goes on his own business, takes a keen interest in Mayriel's divine magic, and can be unsettlingly insightful.

G GragonisCudgel's lieutenant; half-ogre Adversary Journey 005

Gragonis was Cudgel Ironsmile's lieutenant. A captured mercenary said he went out to the woods a few days before the festival battle and came back with gold, and that some of her company had been paid to turn on Vogler. He was the huge half-ogre the party killed on High Hill. They learned his name afterward, from the prisoner.

I Ispin GreenshieldLate adventurer, friend to many Ally Journey 008

Ispin Greenshield was an old human adventurer, remembered fondly by elves and townsfolk alike. His funeral in Vogler drew Roshi and Mayriel onto the road. He asked that it be an evening of stories.

K KedVillager of Bracken Hollow, black-touched Neutral Journey 011

Ked is a villager of Bracken Hollow, a human in his twenties or thirties. He was the one person in Boyd's shelter whose glow carried the black as well as the white and red, which makes him one of the handful the eclipse chooses from. Aratos gave him his word that the Embers would protect him.

L Lucky LeppomantoGuild master of the Dragon's Eye Unknown Journey 008

Lucky Leppomanto is the guild master of the Dragon's Eye. Lucky set Jasper his task: steal the Orb of Dragonkind from the Tower of Wayreth. Jasper has seen Lucky only in a dream and in the river's apparitions.

M Governor MiatGovernor of Kalaman Neutral Journey 008

Governor Miat rules Kalaman and holds firm control of its council. The council granted the people of Vogler refuge on the condition that the party aid the city's military efforts.

P The Pale WomanUnidentified figure Unknown Journey 009

A pale, beautiful woman with pointed ears and long flowing hair who appears to Aeimos, points, and vanishes. He could not tell what she was. She has whispered to him twice and spoken to him once. No one else has seen or heard her, and Aeimos has told no one.

R Mayor Raven Uth VoglerMayor of fallen Vogler Ally Journey 010

Raven Uth Vogler is the mayor of Vogler. She resisted abandoning the town, then told its people the truth about the Red Dragon Army's demand. She now leads the displaced Voglerans camped outside Kalaman, and has named the party honorary Voglerans.

R RobertoSquire at Kalaman Neutral Journey 010

Roberto is a squire in Kalaman's garrison. He walked the courtyard statues with Aratos, and was later cut down by the Emberwake in a sparring match.

S SeredynHead of the Dragon's Eye in Kalaman Neutral Journey 010

Seredyn heads the Dragon's Eye branch in Kalaman from a table at the back of Finn's Bar. He dresses richly, wears gold earrings, and walks the city behind a faint illusion. Lucky had not told him Jasper was coming.

S Sergeant BoydRetired soldier holding Bracken Hollow together Ally Journey 011

Sergeant Boyd is retired from the army and has made Bracken Hollow his home. Since the village closed he has kept its people fed and indoors at the right hours. He pulled Kakashi off a fresh grave, sheltered the party from the wolves, and told them how the moons turn. He says no one should have to choose who is sent up the hill.

S Lord SothFallen Knight of Solamnia Adversary Journey 009

Lord Soth was once a celebrated Knight of Solamnia. He fell through selfishness and personal failure and lives in disgrace under a curse. Aratos has seen him in the river's apparitions, and the party has fought one of his servants, a necromancer corrupting a tomb of knights. The pale woman told Aeimos alone that Soth wishes to corrupt all knights and their legacies, in vengeance for how they portrayed him, until his curse is lifted.

S SunscaleGolden canary Ally Journey 008

Sunscale is a golden canary that first landed on Mayriel's shoulder on the road to Vogler. It had kept company with Fizban; given the choice in Kalaman, it stayed with her.

T Than RookledustGnome tinkerer of Thornwall Keep Ally Journey 007

Than is a frantic gnome tinkerer who kept a workshop at Thornwall Keep. He is scattered and hard to keep on one subject, and useful anyway. He readied the party's boat as Vogler burned and left aboard it with them. Nothing has been said of him since.

Theros FlametongueOrcish warrior with a flaming axe Adversary Journey 007

Theros Flametongue is a green-skinned orcish warrior who rode into Vogler on the dragon machine carrying a flaming axe. Mayriel killed him on the docks. As the boats pulled away, a figure stood over his body, flame passed across him, and he rose.

V Marshal VendriMarshal of Kalaman Ally Journey 010

Marshal Vendri commands Kalaman's military. He acknowledged Aratos as a Knight Radiant, takes the Embers' service through Darrett, and hands out their missions.

W WyhanApothecary; examiner for High Sorcery Neutral Journey 011

Wyhan keeps an apothecary in Kalaman. She read the High Sorcery scrolls Kakashi and Jasper carried and set both to test for the White Robes. Seredyn names her as the best lead to the Tower of Wayreth. She has sent Kakashi a list of things to gather, frostlace above all.
