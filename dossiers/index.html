B Bakaris the ElderLord; nobleman Neutral Journey 008

Lord Bakaris is a nobleman who dresses in red and gold and takes offense when plans are made without him. He prefers a sealed letter to a ride into danger. He reached Kalaman's council ahead of the refugees.

B Bakaris the YoungerYoung nobleman Neutral Journey 008

Bakaris the Younger is a blond young nobleman, son of Lord Bakaris, with more arrogance than grace and very little real authority.

Becklin Uth ViharinRetired Knight of Solamnia Ally Journey 007

Becklin Uth Viharin was a retired Knight of Solamnia living in Vogler, an old friend of Ispin Greenshield and the town's steadiest hand. She believed the party's warnings, gave Roshi Ispin's shield, and held the docks so the boats could leave. She fell into the Vingaard with blades in her back.

C The Charred SwordswomanUnidentified warrior Adversary Journey 007

A tall woman with charred skin, blackened armor and a massive sword, seen through the smoke at Vogler's waterfront. The party does not know her name.

C Sir CthondorKnight Radiant, at rest Ally Journey 010

Sir Cthondor was a Knight Radiant whose tomb lies in a hidden reliquary outside Kalaman. A necromancer serving Lord Soth raised his spirit as a wraith. Freed, he named Aratos and Aeimos children of Cathan and sounded his horn to send the risen dead back to rest.

C Cudgel IronsmileMercenary captain Ally Journey 007

Cudgel Ironsmile is a mercenary captain whose company played the old foe in Vogler's festival battle. Part of that company sold her out to the Red Army. She fought for the town to the end and was last seen kicked through a building on the burning waterfront.

D Darrett HighwaterBecklin's former squire Ally Journey 010

Darrett Highwater was Becklin's young squire in Vogler. He survived the town's fall, accepted her armor as a promise to grow into it, and now stands as the Embers' proxy with Kalaman so they are not formally conscripted. He trains under Marshal Vendri and sleeps among the Voglerans.

F FinbarDragon's Eye contact Neutral Journey 010

Finbar is a member of the Dragon's Eye who can pass unseen. In Vogler he wore the face of Old Man Jenkins at General Goods. In Kalaman he dropped the disguise and walked Jasper to the guild.

FizbanGreen-robed wizard, old friend of Ispin Ally Journey 010

Fizban is a peculiar old wizard in green robes, found half-swallowed by a living sand creature on the road to Vogler. He comes and goes on his own business, takes a keen interest in Mayriel's divine magic, and is more insightful than he lets on.

G GragonisCudgel's lieutenant Adversary Journey 005 · named only

Gragonis was Cudgel Ironsmile's lieutenant. He went into the woods, came back with Red Army gold, and paid part of her company to turn on Vogler. The party has heard his name and has not met him.

I Ispin GreenshieldLate adventurer, friend to many Ally Journey 008

Ispin Greenshield was an old adventurer whose brief human life left lasting marks on elven memory. His funeral in Vogler drew Roshi and Mayriel onto the road. He asked for tales, not tears.

L Lucky LeppomantoGuild master of the Dragon's Eye Unknown Journey 008

Lucky Leppomanto is the guild master of the Dragon's Eye. He bound Jasper to the guild with a coin and set him a task: steal the Orb of Dragonkind from the Tower of Wayreth. The party has seen him only in dream and apparition.

M Governor MiatGovernor of Kalaman Neutral Journey 008

Governor Miat rules Kalaman and holds firm control of its council. He granted the people of Vogler refuge on the condition that the party aid the city's military efforts.

P The Pale Elven WomanUnidentified elven figure Unknown Journey 009

A pale, beautiful elven woman with long flowing hair who appears to Aeimos, points, and vanishes. She has spoken once.

R Mayor Raven Uth VoglerMayor of fallen Vogler Ally Journey 010

Raven Uth Vogler is the mayor of Vogler. She resisted abandoning the town, then told its people the truth and led them onto the boats. She now leads the displaced Voglerans camped outside Kalaman, and has named the party honorary Voglerans.

R RobertoSquire at Kalaman Neutral Journey 010

Roberto is a squire in Kalaman's garrison. He walked the courtyard statues with Aratos, and later took the Emberwake's blade in a sparring match.

S SeredynHead of the Dragon's Eye in Kalaman Neutral Journey 010

Seredyn heads the Dragon's Eye branch in Kalaman from a table at the back of Finn's Bar. He dresses richly, wears gold earrings, and walks the city behind a faint illusion. Lucky had not told him Jasper was coming.

S Lord SothFallen Knight of Solamnia Adversary Journey 009

Lord Soth was once a celebrated Knight of Solamnia. He fell through selfishness and personal failure and lives in disgrace under a curse. He works through servants to corrupt knights and their legacies, and means to continue until the curse is lifted. He has brushed the party's path and has not moved against them.

S SunscaleGolden finch Ally Journey 008

Sunscale is a golden finch that appeared to Mayriel in Vogler. It had kept company with Fizban; given the choice in Kalaman, it stayed with her.

T ThanGnome tinkerer of Thornwall Keep Ally Journey 007

Than is a frantic gnome tinkerer who kept a workshop at Thornwall Keep. He is scattered and hard to keep on one subject, and useful anyway. What became of him after Vogler fell is not known.

Theros FlametongueOrcish warrior with a flaming axe Adversary Journey 007

Theros Flametongue is a green-skinned orcish warrior who rode into Vogler on the dragon machine carrying a flaming axe. Mayriel killed him on the docks. As the boats pulled away, flame passed over his body and he stood up again.

V Marshal VendriMarshal of Kalaman Ally Journey 010

Marshal Vendri commands Kalaman's military. He acknowledged Aratos as a Knight Radiant, takes the Embers' service through Darrett, and hands out their missions.
