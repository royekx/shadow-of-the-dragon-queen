Sela
Kept Bracken Hollow's sickhouse

Sela was a young woman who tended the sick of Bracken Hollow on cots in the house her husband Miren built. She alone moved untouched by the white moon's sickness. Last winter the Pale Fever nearly killed her, and Miren put a white tooth from Marigold around her neck. She recovered. She gave the tooth up to save the village, and at once became a dead thing and lunged at Tien. Tien's ray of frost brought her down.
