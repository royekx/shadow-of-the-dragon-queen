Sergeant Boyd
Retired soldier holding Bracken Hollow together

Sergeant Boyd is a retired soldier pushing sixty, gray-haired and dark-skinned, who has made Bracken Hollow his home. He lived through the Blood Summer there twenty-seven years ago, when hobgoblins besieged the village, and his wife did not survive it. Becklin brought him the banner that flew over the gate after that. While the village was shut he kept its people fed and indoors at the right hours. He fought beside the Embers on the hill, rallied the villagers behind him, and thanked the party when the spell broke. He will always lay down his life for the people he protects.
