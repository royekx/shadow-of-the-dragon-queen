Marigold
Bracken Hollow's healer, and more than that

Marigold was Bracken Hollow's village grandmother, an old healer who kept to herself in a cottage just past its edge. She helped everyone through the Pale Fever last winter, and some said she granted people things. She gave Miren the white tooth that kept Sela alive. While the spell held, her cottage could be seen but not reached. The monument's light took Jasper there twice: she served him tea, wore the monument's three rings in her jewelry, and knew his name without being told. When the monument landed in her garden her face slipped, long and peeling, and she said eighty-four years of feeding was good enough. As the spell broke she told them all that she had got what she needed and that perhaps they would meet again. Then she was gone.
