Fizban
Green-robed wizard, old friend of Ispin

Fizban is a peculiar old wizard in green robes, found half-swallowed by a living sand creature on the road to Vogler. He comes and goes on his own business, takes a keen interest in Mayriel's divine magic, and can be unsettlingly insightful.
