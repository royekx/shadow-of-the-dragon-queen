Lucky Leppomanto
Guild master of the Dragon's Eye

Lucky Leppomanto is the guild master of the Dragon's Eye. He bound Jasper to the guild with a coin and set him a task: steal the Orb of Dragonkind from the Tower of Wayreth. The party has seen him only in dream and apparition.
