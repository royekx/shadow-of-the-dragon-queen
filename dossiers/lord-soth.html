Lord Soth
Fallen Knight of Solamnia

Lord Soth was once a celebrated Knight of Solamnia. He fell through selfishness and personal failure and lives in disgrace under a curse. He works through servants to corrupt knights and their legacies, and means to continue until the curse is lifted. He has brushed the party's path and has not moved against them.
