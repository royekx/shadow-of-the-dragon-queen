Seredyn
Head of the Dragon's Eye in Kalaman

Seredyn heads the Dragon's Eye branch in Kalaman from a table at the back of Finn's Bar. He dresses richly, wears gold earrings, and walks the city behind a faint illusion. Lucky had not told him Jasper was coming.
