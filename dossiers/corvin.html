Corvin
Villager of Bracken Hollow

Corvin is a broad-shouldered man in his forties with a rough beard, one of those who go out under the black moon for food. He told the party what Boyd would not: that another of the black-touched can be sent up the hill in place of the one the eclipse chooses. He believes the village must make the best of that. On the hill he kept Ked from leaving at the point of a dagger.
