Cudgel Ironsmile
Mercenary captain

Cudgel Ironsmile is a mercenary captain whose company played the old foe in Vogler's festival battle. Part of that company sold her out to the Red Army. She fought for the town to the end and was last seen kicked through a building on the burning waterfront.
