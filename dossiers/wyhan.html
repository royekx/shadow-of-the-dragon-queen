Wyhan
Apothecary and mage of High Sorcery

Wyhan keeps an apothecary in Kalaman and is a mage of High Sorcery. She set Kakashi and Jasper to test for the White Robes, and Seredyn names her as the best lead to the Tower of Wayreth.
