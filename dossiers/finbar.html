Finbar
Dragon's Eye contact

Finbar is a kender of the Dragon's Eye who can pass unseen. In Vogler he wore the face of Old Man Jenkins and kept General Goods. Jasper's coin showed him the figure inside the disguise, and before Vogler fell he had the name as well. The rest of the party still knows him only as Jenkins. In Kalaman he walked Jasper to the guild behind a different human face, and showed his own only inside its door.
