Sir Cthondor
Knight of the hidden reliquary, at rest

Sir Cthondor was a knight whose tomb lies in a hidden reliquary of the Knights Radiant outside Kalaman. A necromancer serving Lord Soth raised his spirit as a wraith. Freed, he named Aratos and Aeimos children of Cathan and sounded his horn to send the risen dead back to rest.
