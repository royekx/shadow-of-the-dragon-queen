Kakashi
Wizard in training, testing for the White Robes of High Sorcery

Kakashi is a wizard who keeps a raven familiar. He passed a mage trial beside Jasper and met the others in the ambush on the road to Vogler.

Since the night the moons aligned on his watch he has wanted greater magic, urgently. He reads what others miss: the cipher in Fizban's recipe, the curse on Aratos's axe, the spellwork over Bracken Hollow. Wyhan has set him to test for the White Robes.

PursuitGrowing his power and earning his White Robes, now that the test is postponed for the war, while puzzling over a vision of a tower.
NowHeading west to Kalaman with Roshi and the two travelers, stooped and drained from the working on the hill. He carries everything on Wyhan's list, and Fizban wants his wand.
