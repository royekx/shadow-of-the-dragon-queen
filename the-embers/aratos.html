Aratos Stormblessed
Barbarian of the Ancestral Guardians; a Knight Radiant of Cathan's line

A vision came to Aratos like a summons: scaled warriors marching in ranks, and one command in it, fight. He set out for Kalaman to warn the Knights of Solamnia.

He is a barbarian whose ancestors gather around him when he rages, and a Knight Radiant of Cathan's line, as is his cousin Aeimos. He was first to speak his oath at the reliquary door and first to challenge the monument at Bracken Hollow. He carries the Emberwake, a cursed axe he took as a trophy at Vogler.

PursuitTrying to hold to the path of restraint he chose, after the Emberwake's fury took him at Bracken Hollow.
NowUnconscious on the hill at Bracken Hollow, guarded by the spectral warriors of the horn. The monument marked him with the black glow.
