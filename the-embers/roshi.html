Roshi
Devoted protector carrying Ispin's legacy

Roshi took the road to Vogler for the funeral of Ispin Greenshield. On the way, near the remnants of an old holy place, she felt a sacred calling settle on her.

She puts herself between danger and other people, as she did against the half-ogre at High Hill and the messenger at Vogler's gate. Her hands heal, which no one in living memory had seen. She carries Ispin's green shield and a moon-touched sword.

PursuitLiving up to Ispin's example as a shield for others, and growing into her sacred calling.
NowHurt but standing after the hill at Bracken Hollow. Wears the periapt that kept her alive.
