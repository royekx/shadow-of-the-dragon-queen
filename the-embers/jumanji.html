Jumanji
Bard of Barrowmere; a guest

Jumanji is a very tall, broad bard from the village of Barrowmere, with a flute at his hip. He and Tien were carrying a letter from Barrowmere to Kalaman when they walked into Bracken Hollow and could not walk out. In the week they were shut in, they became two of the people Sergeant Boyd relied on.

A few notes of his tune ease the way into a conversation, and an insult of his can kill. He finished the hobgoblin captain at the gate with words alone, refused the monument's pull on the hill, and seated the red banner beneath it.

PursuitCarrying Barrowmere's letter to Kalaman to ask for help.
