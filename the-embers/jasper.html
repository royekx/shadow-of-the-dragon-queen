Jasper Quickwit
Rogue bound to the Dragon's Eye thieves' guild

Jasper is a rogue who passed a mage trial behind an apprentice's mask. The Dragon's Eye, a thieves' guild, bound him with a deadly coin and set him a task: find the moving Tower of Wayreth and steal the Orb of Dragonkind.

He scouts ahead alone and shoots from cover. He scouted the Red Army camp outside Vogler, saw that the dragon was a machine, and noticed Corvin watching Ked. When Old Man Jenkins packed to flee Vogler, Jasper stayed, saying the town was good and its people needed help.

PursuitCarrying out the task the Dragon's Eye set him: stealing the Orb of Dragonkind from the moving Tower of Wayreth.
NowIn Bracken Hollow with the rest. Lucky's warning from the river still stands: time is running out on the Orb.
