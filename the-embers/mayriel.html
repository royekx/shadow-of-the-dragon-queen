Mayriel
Cleric of Paladine; divine magic returned

Mayriel traveled with Roshi to Ispin's funeral. On the road she saw a vision of divine fire. In Vogler she saw another: a horned woman who became a five-headed dragon, and the platinum fire that shielded her from it.

She heals and fights in the same breath. On the burning docks of Vogler she pledged Paladine her family signet ring, and her obedience when the god asks for it. The Dragon Queen has told her that her sword will find her.

PursuitWalking her divine calling, and waiting for the sword the Dragon Queen has promised will find her.
NowOn the hill at Bracken Hollow, under an eclipse she has seen before. She has had no vision in some time.
