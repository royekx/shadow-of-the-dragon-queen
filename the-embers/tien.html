Tien
Tinkerer of Barrowmere; a guest

Tien is a small tinkerer from the village of Barrowmere, hung all over with gadgets, who works her magic through a set of thimbles. She and Jumanji were carrying a letter from Barrowmere to Kalaman when they walked into Bracken Hollow and could not walk out. In the week they were shut in, they became two of the people Sergeant Boyd relied on.

She found the white tooth at Sela's throat, and when Sela became a dead thing, Tien's ray of frost dropped her. She did not want to kill her. On the hill her glittering dust lit up the monument, her frost cracked its red ring, and she seated the black seed last of the three.
