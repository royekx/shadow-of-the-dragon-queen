Briefing

A bear trail outside Kalaman led to a cave, and the cave to a stone door that asked for an oath. Behind it lay the Radiant Reliquary, the tomb of the knights who fell in the battle shown on its murals. A necromancer serving Lord Soth was raising its dead over a black rose.

Outcome The necromancer and the wraith were destroyed, and the fallen went back to their rest. Aratos carries the horn.
