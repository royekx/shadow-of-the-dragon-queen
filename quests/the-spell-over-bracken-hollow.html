The Spell Over Bracken Hollow

The BakarisesQuest BoardThe Ones Sent Up the Hill
Briefing

Bracken Hollow has been shut for about a month. Nobody can walk out of it, the three moons hang over it in eclipse, and each eclipse takes one of its people. The Embers walked in and are shut in with them.

As he fell on the hill, Kakashi saw a lattice of spellwork laid over the whole village, the true sun beyond it, and three knots in the weave. The monument's three rings were receivers for that working. With all three darkened it could be killed, but what is bound this way can come back unless the binding itself is undone.

Beneath the hill is a closed cavern with three recesses. That is where the spell is bound. Three rings, three recesses, three phases: three things are needed there to break it, and they are somewhere in the village. Kakashi is sure they are something other than the monument's rings.

Outcome The three gifts were seated beneath the hill and the spell broke. Marigold is gone.

Objectives4 of 4

  • Find where the spell is bound
  • Find the three things the spell needs, somewhere in the village
  • Break the spell before the next eclipse
