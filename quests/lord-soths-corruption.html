Lord Soth's Corruption of Knights

Kakashi's Buried PastQuest BoardTheros Flametongue

The pale woman told Aeimos, and only Aeimos, that Lord Soth wishes to corrupt all knights, their image and their legacies, in vengeance for how they portrayed him, and will continue until his curse is lifted. Aeimos told no one before he left. A necromancer in his service was found in the Radiant Reliquary, raising the knights who fell in the battle shown on its murals. A whisper told Aeimos there are more, and that Soth wishes to take them. He has left in the direction the pale woman pointed.
