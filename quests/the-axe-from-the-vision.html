The Axe from the Vision

Wyhan's ListQuest BoardThe Ones Sent Up the Hill
Briefing

Aratos has made his choice. Shown a tree that burns on one side and is glazed with frost on the other, and offered blades bound in fury or an axe marked by cold restraint, he reached for the cold.

The axe from his vision is not somewhere to be found. It is the cursed axe he already carries, changed. At the reliquary the Emberwake became that weapon for a moment, then turned back.

The King, one of the ancestral spirits who appear when he rages, has come to him twice with warnings: the same blood, the same weakness, and that he must test his resolve. What that test will ask of him has not been said.

At Bracken Hollow he did not hold. The fury took him in the middle of a fight, and Roshi had to bring him down.

  • Hold to that choice when the axe's fire rises
