The Ones Sent Up the Hill

The Spell Over Bracken HollowQuest BoardThe Vanishing Fish of Vogler

Each eclipse takes one of the black-touched. Corvin says the village has a choice in it: another of the black-touched can be sent up the hill in the chosen one's place, and that one is taken. He holds that some people's skills are still needed. Boyd says no one should have to make that choice for another. On the hill Corvin kept Ked from leaving at dagger-point, knocked him senseless, and ran. Ked and Aratos had both been marked with the black, and the eclipse was not yet over.

Outcome The spell broke before anyone else was sent up the hill. Corvin and Ked fought on it side by side.
