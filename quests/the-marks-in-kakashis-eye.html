The Marks in Kakashi's Eye

MarigoldQuest BoardThe Emberwake's Curse

At times Kakashi's eyes turn red, split by a black slit. On the hill at Bracken Hollow he watched the monument's spellwork take Jasper, and understood it. Then a second mark opened in his eye, and with it he could cast the working himself. He sent the monument from the hill, but the spell was beyond what his body could bear, and it left him drained for days.
