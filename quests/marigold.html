Marigold

Barrowmere's LetterQuest BoardThe Marks in Kakashi's Eye

Marigold was Bracken Hollow's village grandmother, a healer with a cottage just past its edge. She gave Miren the white tooth that kept Sela alive after the Pale Fever, and some said she granted people things. When the monument's light took Jasper to her cottage, her jewelry carried the monument's three rings and there were clothes in the corner that would fit someone much younger. She knew his name without being told. As the spell failed her face slipped, long and peeling, and she said that eighty-four years of feeding was good enough. When it broke she spoke in all their minds: she had got what she needed, and perhaps they would meet again. Then she was gone.
