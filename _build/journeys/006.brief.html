Stay or Go
  • Kakashi's raven brings word that Cudgel is returning. She arrives with Jeyev, her lieutenant. The army camp lies to the northwest, and her remaining mercenaries are still loyal.
  • She puts it plainly: Vogler can fight, or it can evacuate.
  • Mayor Raven resists leaving. Vogler is home. Becklin agrees that an escape by river must be prepared and thinks an attack is likely.
  • The nearest help is Kalaman and its Knights of Solamnia, a few days away and as long again to return. Cudgel's mercenaries are half a day out.
  • Old Man Jenkins's shop is locked, and no one answers the door.
  • Lord Bakaris the Elder forces his way into the meeting. Aratos and Kakashi flatter him toward riding to Kalaman himself. Bakaris prefers to send a sealed letter and goes to ready his own things.
The Messenger
  • A rider in black and red armor waits at the gate and hands Mayor Raven a scroll from the Red Dragon Army. It names Takhisis, the Dragon Queen.
  • Vogler is to open its doors this very night and quarter the soldiers. Refusal will bring swift and final mercy.
  • Becklin asks how long they have. You have till sunset.
  • Alone at Thornwall Keep, Roshi meets Than, a gnome tinkerer with unstable explosives. He can rig traps and help repair boats, and he warns her off the gnome-flinger on the roof.
  • From the keep she sees armored figures posted on the cliffside.
Telling the Town
  • Mayor Raven tells the people the truth, and panic breaks out. Bakaris presses at her ear to make a decision.
  • Kakashi strikes his staff and commands silence. He tells them there is a plan, sets them to packing and loading boats, and asks those who can fight to stand by.
  • Roshi reports the watchers. Kakashi's raven counts four: three human soldiers and one that looks reptilian.
  • Raven sends townsfolk up to help Than with his explosives.
Buying Time
  • Jeyev has to ride out to gather Cudgel's loyal mercenaries without the watchers seeing.
  • Roshi and Kakashi occupy the messenger. Aratos and Mayariel climb for the watchers on the cliff, and Jasper flies up on his winged boots.
  • Aratos tries to bluff a watcher and shove him off the cliff. He ends up with a blade at his throat.
  • Kakashi asks the messenger to let the women and children leave. Submit peacefully, and there will be no need.
  • Roshi hides Kakashi's casting. A false scream for help turns the rider's head, and Jeyev rides.
Cliff and Gate
  • Mayariel puts one watcher to sleep and burns another to ash with a beam of moonlight.
  • Aratos's axe grows cold in his hand, his swing misses, and he hears the elder warrior's voice say one word. Prove.
  • A watcher who throws red lightning burns Roshi at the gate, strikes Cudgel, and drops Mayariel. Aratos brings her back with a healing potion.
  • Aratos throws his javelin as a bolt of lightning. Jasper shoots one watcher through the skull, and Cudgel crushes another.
  • Roshi challenges the messenger and draws him onto herself. Kakashi's blue lightning sounds like a thousand birds and leaves the rider badly hurt.
  • Jasper looses one of the leeching arrows, and the petrified leech at its tip comes alive.
  • Aratos puts a javelin through the messenger's heart. Jeyev is away.
Before Sunset
  • Among the fallen are two healing potions and gold. One body carries a Periapt of Wound Closure, which preserves its wearer from death, and a blue-gemmed Ring of Protection.
  • The town repairs boats and helps Than set traps. Cudgel's mercenaries take a position north of town.
  • Becklin and Cudgel will hold the front line. The party is to get the villagers out and stand as the last line.
  • Becklin asks Aratos for one thing. Once this settles, he is to bring the chest in Thornwall Keep to Darrett.
