Stay or Go
  • Kakashi's raven brings word from Cudgel. She returns with Jeyev, a loyal lieutenant. The army camp lies to the northwest, and her loyal mercenaries survived the betrayal.
  • She puts it plainly: Vogler can fight, or it can evacuate.
  • Mayor Raven resists leaving. Vogler is home. Becklin agrees that an escape by river must be prepared.
  • Lord Bakaris the Elder forces his way into the meeting. Kakashi flatters him toward carrying word to Kalaman. Bakaris prefers to send a sealed letter.
The Messenger
  • A rider in black and red armor waits at the gate with a scroll from the Red Dragon Army, in the name of the Dragon Queen.
  • Vogler is to open its doors by sunset and quarter the soldiers. Refusal will bring swift and final mercy.
  • At Thornwall Keep, Roshi meets Than, a gnomish tinkerer with unstable explosives. He can rig traps and help repair boats.
  • From the keep she sees more armored figures on the cliff above the gate.
Telling the Town
  • Mayor Raven tells the people the truth, and panic breaks out. Bakaris stirs it from the edge of the crowd.
  • Kakashi strikes his staff and commands silence. He tells them there is a plan and sets them to packing and loading boats.
Buying Time
  • Jeyev has to ride out to gather Cudgel's loyal mercenaries without the watchers seeing.
  • Roshi and Kakashi occupy the messenger. Aratos, Mayriel and Jasper climb for the watchers on the cliff.
  • Aratos tries to bluff a watcher and ends up with a blade at his throat. The fight starts on both fronts, and Jeyev rides.
  • Mayriel calls down a beam of moonlight. Aratos's axe grows cold in his hand, and a voice demands he prove himself.
  • Mayriel is struck down and brought back with a healing potion.
  • Aratos puts a javelin through the messenger's heart. Kakashi's lightning sounds like a thousand birds. Jasper uses one of the leeching arrows.
  • Among the fallen are two healing potions, gold, an amulet that preserves its wearer from death, and a ring of protective magic.
Before Sunset
  • The town repairs boats and helps Than set traps. Cudgel's mercenaries take a position north of town.
  • Becklin and Cudgel will hold the front line. The party is to get the villagers out and stand as the last line.
  • Becklin asks Aratos for one thing. If she does not return, he is to bring the chest in Thornwall Keep to Darrett.
