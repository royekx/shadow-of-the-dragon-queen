Welcome to Vogler
  • The Brass Crab offers food, drink and stories of Ispin Greenshield. Roshi orders crab ramen despite the danger to her health.
  • At Gertrude's smithy, Aratos meets Becklin, a retired Knight of Solamnia who knew Ispin well. He tells her of his vision, and she does not dismiss him.
  • Aeimos, Aratos's cousin, turns up and steals his ring as a prank before showing himself. He agrees to come toward Kalaman.
Mayriel at the Docks
  • Near a fish statue the locals touch for luck, Mayriel has another vision: reptilian soldiers marching on knights, and above them a horned woman in dark regalia who speaks against the followers of Paladine.
  • The woman unfolds into a five-headed dragon. Flames of many colors fall, and platinum fire rises to shield Mayriel.
  • Fizban finds her shaken. If the gods are speaking, he says, she should listen.
Crustdurn Isle
  • Fishermen report poor catches. The statue bears divine traces. A lonely island in the river is said to be haunted.
  • Kakashi sends his raven to scout the abandoned shack there, and the party rows out.
  • A hatch in the shack leads down to a cavern littered with fish bones. Old writings suggest that something below once had to be fed or appeased.
The Beast Beneath
  • The creature wakes: a mass like a great frog, with grasping tentacles.
  • It swallows Roshi. The others have to kill it before it can consume her.
  • Aratos drives lightning through it, Jasper's arrows find their marks, and Aeimos strikes with storm-charged fists. It falls.
  • In the cavern they find a moon-touched sword and a pair of boots marked by strange magic. Aeimos cuts out the creature's tongue as a trophy.
  • Becklin meets them on the mainland, grateful and surprised at what they carry back.
