Welcome to Vogler
  • Vogler is preparing for the Kingfisher Festival, which marks the victory of the Knights of Solamnia over the Istarians. A mock battle is planned for the next day.
  • The Brass Crab offers food, drink and stories of Ispin Greenshield. Roshi orders crab ramen despite the danger to her health.
  • The barkeep tells Kakashi that the fishers have been having trouble.
  • At Gertrude's smithy, Aratos has his weapons sharpened and meets Becklin, a retired Knight of Solamnia. He tells her he carries a warning for her order, and she agrees to hear it later at the Brass Crab.
  • On the way to Thornwall Keep, Jasper sees an older figure wrapped in a strange shimmer. He walks on and tells no one.
  • At the keep, Becklin stops Jasper and tells him Ispin's celebration is that evening.
Mayriel at the Docks
  • Near a fish statue the locals touch for luck, Mayriel has another vision: reptilian soldiers marching on knights, and above them a horned woman in dark regalia.
  • The woman looks at her and speaks. Followers of Paladine will fall to my hand.
  • The woman unfolds into a five-headed dragon. Flames of many colors fall, and platinum fire rises to shield Mayriel. A voice tells her: Worry not, child.
  • Fizban finds her shaken. If the gods are speaking, he says, she should listen.
The Cousins
  • While Aratos is at the smithy, his cousin Aeimos walks in and steals his ring as a prank before showing himself.
  • Aeimos wears blue markings where Aratos's are red.
  • Aratos tells him of his vision and asks him to join the journey to the Knights of Solamnia. Aeimos gives no firm answer. We drink. We see where the night takes us.
Crustdurn Isle
  • Jasper and Mayriel hear an odd sound from the water at the wharf. Fizban sends the others down to join them.
  • Fishermen report poor catches, and one has heard an odd drilling sound at night. Kakashi's spell shows divine traces on the statue, which carries a faded Solamnic inscription.
  • Jasper spots a lonely island in the river. It is said to be haunted, and the locals avoid it.
  • Kakashi sends his raven out over the water and sees an abandoned shack through its eyes.
  • Aeimos pays a fisherman in fresh-caught fish to row the party out. The man agrees to wait three hours. Fizban stays behind.
  • In the shack Jasper finds a page from an old journal. With the inscription, it suggests that something below once had to be fed or appeased.
  • A hatch leads down to a cavern littered with fish bones.
The Beast Beneath
  • The party tries to surround the sleeping creature. Armor clanks and it wakes: a mass like a great frog, with grasping tentacles.
  • It swallows Roshi. The others have to kill it before it can consume her.
  • Aratos drives lightning through it, and Jasper's arrows find their marks.
  • Aeimos breaks its jaw. Aratos hauls on the rope tied to his cousin's ankle, and Aeimos comes down with a last lightning-charged blow. It falls, and Roshi is freed.
  • In the cavern they find a moon-touched sword and a pair of boots of false tracks. Aeimos cuts out the creature's tongue.
Back in Vogler
  • Becklin meets them on the mainland, grateful and surprised at what they carry back.
  • She says she is the only knight in Vogler, and that people keep away from Crustdurn Isle out of superstition.
  • The party shows her the journal page. She knows only of an old man who lived on the island before her time.
  • She invites them to Ispin's celebration at the Brass Crab that evening.
