A List from Wyhan
  • Kakashi calls his raven back on the road. It is the same bird, with the same memory.
  • He carries a list in Wyhan's hand: willowbark, pine resin, graveyard moss, and frostlace, the one that matters.
  • Willowbark comes from a creek and resin from a stand of pines. From the creek, Jasper and Mayriel spot a village in the trees. The sign reads Bracken Hollow.
Three Moons at Once
  • The village is shut and silent. A tall stone monument stands on a hill beyond the houses.
  • The light fails. The white, red and black moons stand together in eclipse, and the forest behind the party is dark too.
  • Mayriel and Roshi last saw that sky a month ago. It should come once a year.
  • Kakashi goes to the graveyard for moss and for bones. Some of the graves are fresh. A gray-haired man in tattered armor pulls him off one: you need to go. Quickly.
Wolves
  • Four black wolves come out of the dark, larger than they should be. Jasper drops the first with an arrow.
  • Aratos opens one from shoulder to flank with the Emberwake. Green flame leaps to the next.
  • Roshi waits inside an illusory griffon and lets a wolf run onto her sword. Jasper's second arrow takes the last through the neck.
  • The old soldier hurries them indoors: there will be more. Kakashi goes back to the grave first and leaves with a set of bones.
What Boyd Told Them
  • Sergeant Boyd, retired from the army, has been holding the village together. Nobody has been able to leave for about a month. Walk out and you walk back in on the far side. A few travelers are shut in with the villagers.
  • White moon: the longest and safest. People feel sick, and echoes of the villagers walk through an ordinary day.
  • Red moon: a battle is fought in the town. The militia died in the first one.
  • Black moon: the wolves, which always come back. It is also the only time the farms give food.
  • The eclipse: about an hour. The monument wakes, and someone is taken. It stays on its hill and does not come into the village.
  • Everyone glows faintly. All carry white, fewer red, and five or six black. One of the black-touched vanishes each eclipse. About forty of two hundred are gone.
  • A healer named Marigold keeps a cottage just outside the village. She was away the night it closed. For frostlace, ask the farmer Gribb Brack.
The Part Boyd Left Out
  • Boyd says no one has been sent up the hill. Corvin, a broad, bearded man at the back: that's not the full truth, now is it, Boyd?
  • When someone is chosen, another of the black-touched can be sent up the hill in their place. Some skills, Corvin says, are still needed to keep the town together.
  • Boyd is angry. Nobody should make that decision for someone else.
  • Jasper sees Corvin watching the one black-touched man in the room, a human in his twenties or thirties, and reads that he wanted Boyd out of the house.
  • The party decides to watch the hill during the eclipse and bring both men. Aratos points his axe at the man: this one. Corvin talks Ked into it, and Aratos promises to protect him with their lives.
The Hill
  • The monument unfolds arms and a head and stands. Three rings burn on its chest: white, red, black.
  • Kakashi raises a skeleton from the bones. Ked is terrified, and his glow brightens as they climb.
  • Aratos demands to know its intent. The black ring flares, and it marks him black. You cannot have me, or any of us.
  • He reaches for a javelin and feels the pull of the Emberwake. Every other weapon sits wrong in his hand.
  • Kakashi strikes first with lightning. Ked backs away, into Corvin's dagger.
The Fight Turns
  • Ordinary weapons do half their work. Roshi's moon-touched sword hums in the light of Solinari, the white moon, and flashes as she strikes. Jasper's spear misses and stays on the hillside.
  • Ked tries to leave and strikes Corvin. Mayriel's bolt leaves the stone glowing.
  • The monument roots itself and its wounds close. Darkness rolls off it. Corvin knocks Ked senseless. Then the moons flash and light hammers the hilltop.
  • Roshi falls, held from death by the periapt. Kakashi falls.
  • Aratos blows the Horn of Sir Cthondor. Five spectral warriors answer and drag the fallen clear. The horn will not answer again for seven days, and the warriors stay an hour.
  • As he falls, Kakashi sees a lattice of spellwork over the whole village, the true sun beyond it, and three knots in the weave.
  • Roshi lays hands on Kakashi, then cries out, and divine light heals them all. The party has one healing potion left.
Three Rings
  • Kakashi wakes shouting: strike the rings. Jasper puts a leeching arrow into the stone. Kakashi's lightning dims the black ring. The next time the darkness rolls out, the monument's wounds stay open.
  • The darkness touches Aratos while he is raging. A fury that is not his own takes him, and the axe's embers become full flame.
  • Corvin runs.
  • Aratos darkens the white ring. A spectral warrior darkens the red.
  • Aratos drags the Emberwake through the earth and takes the monument's head off. The stone runs like lava.
  • He is still lost to the fury, with his friends nearest. Roshi circles wide and drops him with one blow. The spectral warriors take up a defensive position around him.
Beneath the Hill
  • A beam of red, white and black light rises. It comes from inside the hill.
  • Kakashi reads the rings as receivers. The working flows to them from somewhere else, and what is bound that way can return.
  • He and Jasper climb down a crevice at the top of the hill into a closed cavern with three recesses.
  • Three rings, three recesses, three phases. Three things are needed here to break the spell, and they are somewhere in the village. Kakashi is sure they are something other than the rings on the fallen stone.
  • They have until the next eclipse: a little less than a day.
  • This eclipse is not over. Aratos and Ked both lie senseless on the hill.
