A Hunt for Stew
  • The refugees have tents and fresh water outside Kalaman's walls. Food is still a question. Mayor Raven names the party honorary Voglerans.
  • Fizban proposes stew from one of Ispin's old recipes. It needs meat, and the party goes hunting.
  • Aeimos has returned and shadows them unseen until Jasper notices him.
  • Behind Mayriel, Aeimos sees a pale woman with pointed ears. She points toward the others and vanishes.
The Door
  • The bear trail ends at a cave with no bear in it. There is a faint blue glow, and purplish-black soot like the ash from the river.
  • A stone door bears the sword of the Knights Radiant, with six points instead of four. It wants an oath.
  • Aratos speaks his: life before death, strength before weakness, journey before destination. The word Valor lights.
  • Aeimos speaks his: hopelessness is not my privilege. The word Resolve lights.
  • Roshi offers hers, to strive for the best and for the greater good. Foresight lights, and the door opens.
The Reliquary
  • Murals show Knights of Solamnia and Knights Radiant together beneath a floating city, then the city falling in flames. One knight carries a horn. Someone has smeared the mural with the same ash.
  • Below is a tomb of knights. A withered necromancer chants over a spectral black rose in front of the knight with the horn. Two of the dead have already risen.
  • Kakashi's lightning, Jasper's shots and Aeimos's gauntlets open the fight.
  • Aratos rages, and three spectral figures stand with him: a towering bald warrior, a kingly hooded figure, and a slim blue-hued guardian with a spear.
  • Roshi charges, and rose-thorns of radiant power burst from her blade.
  • The necromancer floods the chamber with darkness. Jasper's shot breaks his concentration.
The Wraith
  • The necromancer dies speaking in service to Lord Soth. The rose's power pours into the central knight, and he rises as a wraith.
  • Fear roots most of the party. Aeimos sees that the ritual was incomplete and that radiant power will hurt the wraith most.
  • Mayriel's sacred flame burns it. Kakashi pushes the Boilerdrake Heart past its limits to add fire to his lightning.
  • Mayriel's spiritual weapon, shaped like a rose, closes its petals around the wraith and ends it.
Sir Cthondor
  • The knight's spirit stands as he was in life. He thanks the party and names Aratos and Aeimos children of Cathan.
  • He blows his horn, and the risen knights return to rest.
  • The necromancer leaves an armor of bone. Aeimos blows the horn from the central statue. It makes no sound, and two spectral knights appear. The knight's name is on the tomb: Sir Cthondor.
  • The party finds a bear after all. The camp eats Ispin's stew that night.
Afterward
  • Aeimos leaves the horn with Aratos and slips away.
  • Jasper finds a note in Thieves' Cant, marked with the Dragon's Eye: We need to talk.
  • The pale woman appears to Aeimos again. Lord Soth, she says, seeks to corrupt all knights until his curse is lifted. She points into the distance and vanishes.
