A Hunt for Stew
  • The party brings the council's answer to Mayor Raven. The refugees have tents and fresh water outside Kalaman's walls. Food is still a question.
  • Raven names the party honorary Voglerans. Told that Lord Bakaris is already at work in the city, she rolls her eyes and says she will try to deal with it.
  • Fizban proposes Ispin's famous stew. It needs meat, and the party goes hunting in the hills.
  • Aeimos has returned from the road with Fizban and shadows the party. Only Jasper notices, and the two trade a nod.
  • Behind Mayriel, Aeimos sees a pale woman with pointed ears. She points toward the others and vanishes.
  • Aeimos tells the party that something is wrong. He does not mention the woman.
The Door
  • The bear trail ends at a cave with no bear in it. There is a pulsing blue glow. Mayriel sees purplish-black soot like the flames from the river.
  • A stone door bears the sword of the Knights Radiant, with six points instead of four. It wants an oath.
  • Aratos whispers his: life before death, strength before weakness, journey before destination. The word Valor lights.
  • Aeimos says his under his breath: hopelessness is not my privilege. The word Resolve lights.
  • Kakashi works out that the door wants the virtues of a knight. Six symbols show, and four are correct.
  • Aeimos presses his head to the stone and says this too shall pass. A third spoke lights.
  • Kakashi touches the door and offers what drives him: always to strive to do the best, and for the greater good. Foresight lights. Roshi thinks of her oath to leave the world better than she found it, and the door opens.
The Reliquary
  • Murals show Knights of Solamnia and Knights Radiant fighting together against a floating city, then the city falling in flames.
  • One of the two leaders sounds a horn. Someone has smeared his image with the same ash.
  • A whisper tells Aeimos to go forward. He turns left, down a broken stair.
  • Below is a round tomb of knights. A withered necromancer chants over a spectral black rose near the knight with the horn. Two of the dead have already risen.
The Necromancer
  • Kakashi's lightning, Jasper's arrow and Aeimos's gauntlets open the fight. Ordinary weapons bite less than they should.
  • Aratos rages, and three spectral figures stand with him: a towering bald warrior, a kingly hooded figure, and a slim blue-hued guardian with a spear. The Emberwake feels heavier than it should.
  • Roshi charges with her greatsword, and rose-thorns of radiant power burst from the blade.
  • The necromancer floods the chamber with darkness and tries to slip away. Aratos holds him in place.
  • Jasper's shot breaks the spell. A third knight has risen, and the one Aeimos felled is standing again.
  • Aeimos beats the necromancer down. His last words: His will is done, Lord Soth.
The Wraith
  • The rose's power pours into the central knight, and he rises as a wraith. Its scream leaves most of the party unable to approach it.
  • Aeimos sees that the rose had room for five petals and held three, one for each knight raised. The ritual was cut short, and radiant power will hurt the wraith most.
  • Mayriel's sacred flame burns it. The wraith's touch drains Jasper and Mayriel.
  • Roshi holds the wraith close with a champion's challenge. Kakashi pushes the Boilerdrake Heart past its limits to add fire to his lightning.
  • The wraith tells Aeimos: Your light will fade.
  • Mayriel turns the dead knights. Her spiritual weapon, shaped like a rose, closes its petals around the wraith and ends it.
Sir Cthondor
  • The knight's spirit stands as he was in life. He thanks the party and names Aratos and Aeimos children of Cathan.
  • He blows his horn, and the three risen knights return to rest.
  • The necromancer leaves an armor of bone, which Kakashi reads as necrotic. Aratos takes the head to show the city.
  • Aeimos blows the horn from the central statue. It makes no sound, and two spectral knights appear.
  • The name on the statue is Sir Cthondor.
  • Outside, a whisper reaches Aeimos alone: There are more. He wishes to take them.
Afterward
  • The party finds a bear after all. The camp eats Ispin's stew that night, and Darrett joins late from training.
  • Kakashi asks Fizban to talk after dinner. Fizban says he will be in Kalaman only another day or so.
  • Aeimos leaves the horn with Aratos and slips away.
  • Jasper finds a note in Thieves' Cant, marked with the Dragon's Eye: We need to talk.
  • Alone beyond the camp, Aeimos meets the pale woman again. Lord Soth, she says, wishes to corrupt all knights in vengeance for how they portrayed him, until his curse is lifted.
  • She points into the distance and vanishes. Aeimos has told no one about her.
