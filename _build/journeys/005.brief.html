After High Hill
  • Militia and mercenaries lie dead across the hill. Survivors are carried into town under the festival banners.
  • One traitor is alive. Cudgel wants answers badly. Becklin keeps her composure.
  • Becklin says assassins also went after the town's leaders and Cudgel.
  • Becklin asks the party to stay. They agree to remain until Vogler can stand on its own.
The Prisoner
  • At the Brass Crab, Cudgel nearly beats the prisoner. Mayor Raven steps in. Aratos makes the threat clear in a low voice, and the man breaks.
  • He names Cudgel's lieutenant, Gragonis, who went to the woods days ago and came back with gold.
  • Gragonis is the big one: the half-ogre the party killed on High Hill.
  • The job was to kill the militia and take Vogler. Cudgel was in the way.
  • Cudgel goes back to her camp alone that night to see who is still loyal. She promises to return.
  • Kakashi's raven follows her at a distance.
Aratos's Vision
  • Aratos dozes before the scouts leave and sees a colossal tree split between flame and frost.
  • A stern, ancient figure at its base offers him twin chained blades bound in fury and vengeance, or an axe marked by cold restraint. Choose, boy. The gods are watching.
  • He wakes chilled, with a pull in one definite direction.
The Camp in the Woods
  • Aratos, Kakashi, Jasper and Darrett scout the woods to the northeast. Roshi and Mayriel stay to guard the town and rest.
  • Jasper goes ahead alone and finds one to two hundred armed soldiers, organized and well supplied.
  • A patrol heads for his companions. Jasper's thrown rock draws a guard onto him instead.
  • Out of the others' sight, he uses the Dragon's Eye coin to pass as a soldier relieving himself.
  • Aratos creeps close and sees a broad figure built like the creatures from the road. The others do not see it.
  • Kakashi hides himself and Darrett under an illusory boulder until the figure leaves.
Among the Wounded
  • Roshi lays hands on an injured fisherman. He asks whether she has been touched by the gods and says nothing like it has been seen in centuries.
  • Roshi thinks on Ispin and his words. A silhouette slips from the edge of her sight and leaves a flower.
  • Mayriel follows a canary to a woman hiding a fatal wound, and heals her with golden light. The woman has seen no bird.
  • Asked whether the gods have touched her, Mayriel says yes. A voice answers her afterward. Good.
  • Mayriel urges Mayor Raven to fortify. The nearest help is two days away.
The Town Prepares
  • The scouts report to Mayor Raven and Becklin. Vogler sets watches, posts hunters as lookouts and strengthens what defenses it can.
  • Jasper, alone, finds Old Man Jenkins packing to flee. Jenkins calls him a fool for staying and asks him to tell no one.
  • Jenkins hands over two healing potions and leech arrows with dried leeches fixed to the heads.
  • His parting words give Jasper a name. Remember, old Finbar always takes care of you.
  • Cudgel has not returned, and neither has Kakashi's raven. The party stays.
