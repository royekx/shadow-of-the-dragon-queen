After High Hill
  • Militia and mercenaries lie dead across the hill. Survivors are carried into town under the festival banners.
  • One traitor is alive. Cudgel wants answers badly. Becklin keeps her composure.
  • At the Brass Crab, Cudgel nearly beats the prisoner. Mayor Raven steps in. Aratos makes the threat clear in a low voice, and the man breaks.
  • He says Cudgel's lieutenant, Gragonis, went into the woods and came back with gold. Some of the mercenaries had been paid to turn on Cudgel and slaughter the militia.
  • Cudgel goes back to her camp alone to see who is still loyal. Kakashi's raven follows at a distance.
Aratos's Vision
  • Aratos sees a colossal tree split between flame and frost.
  • A stern, ancient figure at its base offers him blades bound in fury and vengeance, or an axe marked by cold restraint. Choose, boy. The gods are watching.
The Camp in the Woods
  • Aratos, Kakashi, Jasper and Darrett scout the woods. Roshi and Mayriel stay with the wounded.
  • Jasper goes ahead alone and finds one to two hundred armed soldiers, organized and well supplied.
  • A patrol comes close. Jasper uses the Dragon's Eye coin to pass as one of the guards.
  • The patrols include larger, more bestial figures like the creatures from the road.
Among the Wounded
  • Roshi lays hands on an injured fisherman. He asks whether she has been touched by the gods.
  • Mayriel follows a small bird to a woman hiding a fatal wound, and heals her with golden light.
The Town Prepares
  • The scouts report to Mayor Raven and Becklin. Vogler sets watches and strengthens what defenses it can.
  • Jasper finds Old Man Jenkins packing to flee. Jasper's choice to stay surprises him, and he hands over two healing potions and arrows that leech life.
  • Cudgel has not returned. The party chooses to stay.
