Stew and a Scrap of Parchment
  • The camp is lighter after Fizban's stew. The party shares out what it carried from the reliquary, and Kakashi takes the Armor of the Fallen.
  • Darrett comes in late from his new duties and sleeps among the Voglerans. Fizban says he may be in Kalaman another day or so, learning what is going on outside the city.
  • A scrap of parchment falls from where Fizban sat. Roshi picks it up: Ispin's campfire stew, recorded by Fizban, with a reminder at the bottom. Do not forget the fire, the blade, or the green bits.
  • Roshi reads a name in the reminder: green flame blade. Every ingredient carries an absurd number, and Kakashi suspects the numbers are not measurements. Wizards protect their spells.
The King in the Fire
  • Aratos sharpens his weapons alone. The campfire bends into a great tree, burning on one side and glazed with frost on the other. A black rose blooms at its base and vanishes.
  • A figure in a dark green hood waits beyond it: the King, one of his ancestral spirits. The same blood flows through my veins. The same weakness. Don't let it take you.
  • Aratos thinks of his ancestor Cathan Twice-Born, whose failure brought ruin. The Emberwake warms in his hand.
  • The figure fades and leaves no mark in the grass. Aratos tells no one.
Finn's Bar
  • A note in Jasper's pocket says they need to talk. He spots Finbar outside the camp, unseen by the rest. The others watch Jasper leave for the city and cannot make out who he goes with.
  • Finbar drops his invisibility and shows a human face that is no longer Old Man Jenkins. He shows his kender face only inside the guild's door.
  • The sign reads Peller's Provisions. Looked at closely, it reads Finn's Bar. Three knocks at a back door and a passphrase: none shall hide from the Dragon's Eye.
  • At the back sits the man who shimmered with illusion in the council chambers. Lucky told him nothing about Jasper. He sees through Jasper's misdirection and hears the truth of the Orb task.
  • He judges that Lucky must be furious to set a task so reckless. He does not know where the Tower of Wayreth is. He names Wyhan as the best lead and warns that getting in may prove easier than getting out.
  • His eye twitches when Lucky is named. He answers only to Lucky, and Jasper answers to him while in Kalaman: keep it short, cause no trouble, let nothing come back to him.
  • Asked his name at the last, he gives it: Seredyn, head of the Kalaman branch.
The Watches
  • Wyhan turns Kakashi away from her dark shop until morning. He puts on the Armor of the Fallen, and it fits itself to him and binds to his soul. If he dies, it is lost.
  • Mayor Raven tells Aratos the camp has no scouts. He asks a bird to warn them of danger.
  • Jasper tells Roshi only that he visited an old friend. She can tell he is not telling her everything, and keeps the thought to herself.
  • Mayariel tells Aratos she has had no new vision since the river, and that the silence feels suspicious. Aratos wonders whether the promised sword could be a person, even the party itself.
  • Aratos tells her of ancestors who were given visions of warning and did nothing, and of two paths, fire and ice. He does not feel worthy of either.
  • On the last watch Kakashi meets Darrett leaving for the castle in Becklin's armor. Then he sees three full moons in a pail of water. An elven woman takes the place of his reflection, descends a circular stair to a room of altars, and begins to ward what rests on them.
  • She looks at him: you have to fight. Keep them safe. Fire. The vision ends, and he tells no one.
The Apothecary
  • In the morning Aratos goes to the castle to train. The other four go to Wyhan's Apothecary.
  • Wyhan sells Kakashi components to restore his familiar. Scrolls are beyond his purse. Her advice is to find a job.
  • Kakashi recites the recipe's numbers from memory and keeps the paper hidden. Wyhan judges that they decipher part of the recipe into an incantation. Roshi takes the paper back and counts out the words.
  • Mayariel buys two healer's kits. Potions are priced beyond the party's thin purse.
  • Kakashi asks about work. Wyhan allows that she could use a hand or two, and a gust of wind shows them out.
The Axe Takes Hold
  • Aratos spars with the squire Roberto in the castle training yard, first to three hits. Aratos lands two, grows careless, and Roberto lands one.
  • Heat rises in Aratos. He drops the practice sword and goes for the Emberwake. Flames curl on the blade, and everyone around him is a threat. He swings.
  • Roberto falls. Marshal Vendri is thrown off. Darrett catches the axe arm, and when the rage turns on him he holds on and begs Aratos to stop.
  • The rage recedes. Roberto is alive and unconscious. Vendri hears Aratos out, hand on his blade, and overlooks it for the moment.
  • Aratos asks Darrett to carry the axe, then takes it back before he knows he has moved. He thinks of throwing it away and cannot part with it.
  • Vendri tells him to cool off and to bring his party when they are available.
Green Flame
  • Aratos rejoins the others outside the apothecary. Roshi speaks the incantation: blade made bright, let emerald fire arise, strike one foe, then leap before their eyes.
  • Green flame leaps from the page to each of the five and the parchment burns away. All of them now know the cantrip Green Flame Blade.
  • Aratos tells them everything. They should not trust him with the axe, he says, so he must trust them.
  • Kakashi's assessment: the axe is cursed, and a spell to lift it most likely lies with clerics and paladins.
  • Roshi offers to hold the axe. Aratos reaches out and pulls it back.
  • The party agrees on safeguards: no more sparring, Aratos goes nowhere alone, and a spell of helpless laughter stands ready.
  • Among his companions Aratos sees the King again. Don't let it overtake you.
The First Mission
  • The party goes to Marshal Vendri's office at the castle. Roberto is stabilized and a little shaken.
  • Vendri has a mission. Darrett has told him the dragon at Vogler was a machine, and a tinkerer named Tatina Rookledust, a few days from Kalaman, may be able to give some insight into it.
  • Aratos says a Knight Radiant can hold no allegiance to a single order. Darrett offers to stand as the party's proxy and bear any obligation. Vendri accepts.
  • The party takes the stipend it had declined: five gold a week each. It gives its name for the record: the Embers.
  • Vendri promises to see what Kalaman has for a cursed weapon, and sends them off with two healing potions and a map.
Resolve at the Reliquary
  • Before taking the road, Aratos asks to return to the reliquary. His companions will not let him go alone.
  • He stands before the statue with the Emberwake and asks for help overcoming the curse, so he can keep his oaths as a Knight Radiant.
  • The King appears and lays a hand on his shoulder. You must test your resolve.
  • The fire comes at once. Aratos remembers the tree, the fire and the ice, and chooses resolve.
  • The flames die. A chill runs into the weapon, and for a moment he holds the Leviathan Axe. Then it is the Emberwake again.
  • His companions stand ready with weapons drawn. He thinks he can control it, and he will have to be ready.
  • The Embers take the road toward Tatina Rookledust with the map in hand.
