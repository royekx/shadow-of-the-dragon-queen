Stew and a Scrap of Parchment
  • The camp is lighter after Fizban's stew. Darrett comes in late from training and sleeps among the Voglerans rather than behind Kalaman's walls.
  • A scrap of parchment falls from where Fizban sat. Roshi picks it up: Ispin's campfire stew, in Fizban's hand, with every ingredient in an absurd quantity.
  • Kakashi suspects the numbers are not measurements. Wizards hide spells in plain sight.
The King in the Fire
  • Aratos sharpens his weapons alone. The campfire bends into a great tree, burning on one side and glazed with frost on the other. A black rose blooms at its base and vanishes.
  • A figure in a dark green hood waits beyond it: the King, one of his ancestral spirits. The same blood flows through my veins. The same weakness. Don't let it take you.
  • The figure fades and leaves no mark in the grass. The Emberwake warms faintly in Aratos's hand.
Finn's Bar
  • Jasper catches a shimmer at the edge of camp. Finbar drops his invisibility, and the face of Old Man Jenkins with it, and leads him into the city.
  • The sign reads Peller's Provisions. Looked at closely, it reads Finn's Bar. Three knocks and a passphrase.
  • At the back sits Seredyn, head of the Dragon's Eye in Kalaman, the same man who shimmered with illusion in the council chambers. Lucky told him nothing about Jasper.
  • Seredyn sees through Jasper's misdirection and hears the truth of the Orb task. He names Wyhan as the best lead to the Tower of Wayreth, and warns that getting in may prove easier than getting out.
  • His eye twitches when Lucky is named. His instruction: keep it short, cause no trouble, let nothing come back to him.
  • Jasper tells Roshi only that he visited an old friend. She can tell he is holding something back.
The Watches
  • On watch together, Mayriel tells Aratos she has had no vision in some time and that the silence feels suspicious. She does not know whether the promised sword is a weapon or the one who wields it.
  • Aratos tells her of the ancestor who failed to heed a warning and brought ruin, and of the two paths pulling at him. He does not feel worthy of either.
  • Deep in the night, Kakashi sees three full moons in a pail of water when none are in the sky. An elven woman with frightened eyes takes the place of his reflection, descends a circular stair to a room of altars, and begins to ward what rests on them.
  • She looks up at him: you have to fight. Keep them safe. Fire. The vision ends.
Green Flame
  • At her apothecary, Wyhan sells Kakashi components to restore his familiar and confirms the recipe is a cipher. The spell is Green Flame Blade.
  • Roshi and Mayriel buy healer's kits. Potions are priced beyond the party's thin purse.
  • Outside, Roshi speaks the incantation: blade made bright, let emerald fire arise, strike one foe, then leap before their eyes.
  • Green flame leaps from the page to each of the five and the parchment burns away. All of them now know the cantrip.
The Weight of the Axe
  • Aratos spars with the squire Roberto in the castle training yard. Roberto lands one clean hit.
  • Heat rises in Aratos. The practice sword is on the ground and the Emberwake is in his hand, flames on the blade, and everyone around him is a threat. He swings.
  • Roberto falls. Marshal Vendri and Darrett hold Aratos until the rage recedes. Roberto is alive, unconscious and badly hurt.
  • Vendri hears him out and lets it pass, for now. His eyes stay on the axe.
  • Aratos cannot hand the axe to Darrett. He sets it down and walks back to it without deciding to.
  • Kakashi's assessment: a binding like this takes divine magic to break. Roshi and Mayriel understand what that means.
The Cold Side of the Tree
  • The party returns to the reliquary of Sir Cthondor. Aratos kneels before the statue and asks the honored dead to show him how to carry the axe without being consumed by it.
  • The King appears and lays a hand on his shoulder. You must test your resolve.
  • The fire comes at once. Aratos does not fight it with strength. He thinks of the tree and reaches for the cold side.
  • The flames die. A chill runs into the weapon, and for a moment the Emberwake becomes something else entirely: the weapon from his vision. Then it is the axe again.
  • He thinks he can do it. He will need his companions when he cannot.
The First Mission
  • Marshal Vendri has a mission. A tinkerer named Tatina Rookledust lives several days from Kalaman and may be able to explain the mechanical dragon that burned Vogler.
  • Darrett offers again to stand as the party's proxy so they are not formally conscripted. Vendri accepts and asks for a name for the record.
  • The party gives it: the Embers.
  • Vendri promises to look into remedies for a cursed weapon and sends them off with two healing potions.
  • The Embers walk out through Kalaman's gates toward midday. Fizban will stay in the city a little longer.
