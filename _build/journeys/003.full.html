Vogler welcomed the travelers with the warmth of a town that knew how to mourn and celebrate in the same breath. Children ran through preparations for the Kingfisher Festival, villagers spoke with easy familiarity, and the Brass Crab offered food, drink, and stories. Roshi sought out crab ramen despite the obvious danger to her health, turning the meal into an act of stubborn devotion to memory and appetite. Everywhere, people spoke fondly of Ispin Greenshield, recounting impossible tales of goblins, owlbears, mountains, and narrow escapes, each story half legend and half truth. The barkeep told Kakashi that the festival marked the victory of the Knights of Solamnia over the Istarians, and that the fishers had been having trouble.

Aratos visited Gertrude's smithy to have his weapons sharpened and met Becklin, the retired Knight of Solamnia who counted Ispin a friend. He told her he had come to speak with the knights of her order about a foreboding he wished to share and troubling tidings he believed were on their way. Becklin did not dismiss him. She had matters to tend to first, so they agreed to talk at the Brass Crab.

Jasper walked alone toward Thornwall Keep. On the way he saw an older figure wrapped in a strange shimmer. He kept walking and told no one. At the keep Becklin stopped him and introduced herself. She told him Ispin's celebration would be held that evening and that a mock battle was planned for the next day. Afterward he learned from the fishers that the catch had been poor and that one of them had heard an odd drilling sound from the water at night.

At the docks, Mayriel's day shifted sharply. Near a fish statue touched by locals for luck, she was struck by another vision. She saw a battlefield beneath the moons, reptilian soldiers like those the party had already fought marching against knights, and above them a horned woman in dark regalia commanding slaughter. That figure looked at Mayriel and said that the followers of Paladine would fall to her hand, then unfolded into the terrible shape of a five-headed dragon. Flames of many colors descended, only for platinum fire to rise in answer and shield Mayriel from annihilation, and a voice told her: worry not, child. When the vision ended, Fizban found her shaken and reminded her that if the gods were speaking, she should listen.

Aratos also found family in Vogler. While he was still at the smithy, his cousin Aeimos walked in wearing blue markings where Aratos's were red, and had the easy audacity to steal Aratos's ring as a prank before revealing himself. Their reunion carried the warmth of old bonds and the roughness of shared blood. Aratos told Aeimos of his vision and the danger ahead, asking him to join the journey to the Knights of Solamnia. Aeimos gave no firm answer. He said that since Aratos was here they would drink and see where the night took them.

Jasper found Mayriel at the wharf, where both heard an odd sound from the water, and Fizban sent the others down from the Brass Crab to join them. As the group compared what they had learned, the strange sounds near the river became impossible to ignore. Fishermen reported poor catches, Kakashi's spell showed that the statue bore divine traces, and a faded inscription in Solamnic ran along its base. Then Jasper spotted a lonely island in the river. Kakashi sent his raven out over the water and, looking through its eyes, found an abandoned shack there. The island was rumored to be haunted, and the locals avoided it. Aeimos caught fish with a cast net from the dock and used them to pay a fisherman, who rowed the party out to Crustdurn Isle and agreed to wait three hours. Fizban stayed behind.

Inside the abandoned shack, the party discovered signs of something below. Jasper found a page from an old journal and felt a draft from under the floor. Taken with the inscription on the statue, the old writings suggested that something beneath the island had once needed to be fed or appeased. Aeimos tied a rope to his ankle, handed the other end to Aratos, and went first. A hatch led them down into a cavern beneath the island, where the air was damp, the ground littered with fish bones, and the sound of a creature echoed through the dark. The party tried to surround the sleeping thing, but armor clanked, and the creature woke.

The monster that rose against them was a grotesque thing of frog-like mass and grasping tentacles. Kakashi struck first with a burst of ice, Jasper disappeared into shadows and punished openings, Aratos and Aeimos brought steel and lightning, Mayriel channeled divine harm, and Roshi charged into danger. The creature swallowed Roshi, turning the battle into a frantic effort to kill it before it could consume her. Aratos drove lightning through its body, Jasper's arrows found killing angles, and Aeimos leapt into the fray with storm-charged fists. He broke the creature's jaw, and Roshi could be seen inside. Aratos hauled on the rope still tied to his cousin's ankle, and Aeimos came down on the beast with a last lightning-charged blow. Together, the Stormblessed cousins and their companions brought the creature down, and Roshi was freed.

Within the cavern, a glint led them to a moon-touched sword and an unworn pair of boots of false tracks. Aeimos cut out the creature's tongue, and the party returned to the mainland with proof that the danger beneath Crustdurn Isle had been real. Becklin met them with gratitude and surprise, especially at the monstrous remains they carried back. She was the only knight in Vogler, she said, and people kept away from Crustdurn Isle out of superstition. The party showed her the journal page. She did not know who had written it and could tell them only of an old man who had lived on the island before her time. Then she invited them to the celebration for Ispin that evening. The party made their way to the Brass Crab, which stood ready for the celebration.
