The Night of the Eye
  • Aratos dreams of scaled warriors marching in ranks beneath an eclipsed red moon. He stands his ground, and a voice answers: Good. Fight.
  • He wakes with the moons still aligned and sets out for Kalaman to warn the Knights of Solamnia.
  • Kakashi and Jasper, strangers to each other, take the entry exam of the Mages of High Sorcery at the Barb.
  • Jasper feels his way through an invisible maze and finds a spell of opening carved in the runes. Kakashi confirms it and suspects the key is a trap. They leave the key and open the door with the spell.
  • A red-robed proctor gives them a moon-marked spellbook and a scroll that sends them east to Kalaman.
Two Caravans
  • Kakashi and Jasper ride a caravan east. Kakashi keeps one eye open. Aratos rides the same caravan, and the three barely speak.
  • Roshi and Mayriel each receive a letter from Becklin Uth Viharin: Ispin Greenshield has died, and Vogler will hold his memorial on the eve of the Kingfisher Festival. They meet on a caravan bound for Vogler.
  • One night the two elves follow strange sounds to the remnants of an old holy place.
  • The statue of a silver dragon rebuilds itself before Mayriel. A voice promises her power against a coming evil, and platinum flames rise around her.
  • A great bison statue speaks to Roshi: Take up arms. Evil is coming. Gather a party.
  • Their memory of the night blurs afterward.
The Ambush
  • Mayriel's longbow hums, and a scream follows. Reptilian humanoids with scaled hides, flat noses and long jaws ambush the elves' caravan. They speak Draconic.
  • A knight falls with an arrow in his throat. Soon every knight in the caravan is dead.
  • Aratos, Kakashi and Jasper hear the screams from a distance and run in.
  • Jasper strikes from hiding. Kakashi throws lightning. Roshi grapples one of the creatures and demands to know who sent it.
  • Mayriel alone understands its answer: The Shadow Queen sent us.
  • Aratos knows these soldiers from his dream and charges. He fells their archer, then their leader, which Mayriel has wrapped in fire shaped like a dragon.
  • Roshi's captive tears free. Kakashi's raven carries his lightning to it, and Roshi tackles it as it runs.
The Prisoner
  • The last creature is taken alive. The others learn what Mayriel understood, and Aratos tells them his dream and asks for their help.
  • The creature answers only in Draconic. Mayriel translates.
  • It speaks of reclaiming the realm for its queen, says the east has already fallen, and warns that the shadow is coming fast. The rumors of war in the east are true.
  • Asked whether more of its kind are near, it swallows its own tongue and dies.
The Road to Vogler
  • Two travelers and a young man who ran for help are all that remain of the caravan. One of them asks the five for protection as far as Vogler.
  • Vogler lies on the way to Kalaman. All five agree to go.
  • They bury the fallen, burn the scaled bodies, and take the road together.
