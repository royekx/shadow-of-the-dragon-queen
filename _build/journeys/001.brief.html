Three Roads
  • Aratos wakes from a dream of scaled warriors marching in ranks, with one command in it: fight. It points him toward Kalaman and the Knights of Solamnia.
  • Kakashi and Jasper pass a mage trial together. Jasper reads the runes for the word that opens the door. Kakashi sees through the too-inviting key. They are given a moon-marked spellbook.
  • Roshi and Mayriel travel to Vogler for the funeral of Ispin Greenshield. Near the remnants of an old holy place, Mayriel sees a vision of divine fire and draconic terror, and Roshi feels a sacred calling settle on her.
The Ambush
  • Reptilian humanoids with scaled hides and sharp claws ambush a caravan. They speak Draconic. A knight falls with an arrow in his throat.
  • Jasper strikes from hiding. Kakashi throws lightning. Roshi grapples one of the creatures and demands answers. Aratos knows these soldiers from his dream and charges.
  • Mayriel understands their speech when one of them names the Shadow Queen.
The Prisoner
  • The last creature is taken alive. It speaks of reclaiming the realm for its queen, says the east has already fallen, and warns that the shadow is coming fast.
  • Then it chokes itself to death rather than say more.
  • The five bury the fallen knights, burn the scaled bodies before their decay can spread, and take the road to Vogler together.
