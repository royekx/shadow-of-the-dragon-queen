After the Ambush
  • Roshi digs graves for the fallen knights and sees to the dead.
  • Aratos recognizes the armor of the Knights of Solamnia, the order he set out to find. A briefing on one body says the knights were sent to look into attacks on local farmers.
  • The party takes weapons, coin, and two healing potions from the dead.
  • The scaled bodies decay with disturbing speed. The party burns them.
Night Watches
  • On the first watch Roshi thinks of Ispin, the human adventurer she traveled with and visited in Vogler every few years.
  • Jasper dreams of the Dragon's Eye, the guild that bound him with a coin and a mark on his hand, and of its guildmaster, a man he once conned with a cursed ring.
  • His task is to join the mages and become one of them, and so reach the moving Tower of Wayreth, which holds the Orb of Dragonkind. If he runs, the guild will find him. If he fails, the mark will kill him.
  • None shall hide from the Dragon's Eye. Jasper keeps the dream to himself.
  • On the second watch a golden canary lands on Mayriel's shoulder, greets her, and flies away.
  • Alone on the last watch, Kakashi sees the three moons merge into one shape. Three voices speak: You are not strong enough. Not yet. You need more.
  • He sees archmages bending the elements, summoning titanic beasts, and shaping time itself. He wants greater magic, urgently.
The Man in the Sand
  • One of the elves spots a human foot sticking out of the road. Jasper creeps up and says hello, and the ground starts to swallow his feet as well.
  • A living mass of sand rises around them. Jasper's spear opens it and shows an old man upside down inside. Well met.
  • A tendril of sand pierces Roshi's chest and she falls. Mayriel stops her dying.
  • The old man hauls himself out with help from Kakashi's raven and an offered spear. Kakashi recognizes his incantation, revives Roshi with a healing potion, and drags her clear.
  • The old man freezes the creature solid with a cone of frost. Aratos puts a javelin of lightning through it, and the old man shatters it with a burst of air.
Fizban
  • The old man is Fizban, a wizard in patchwork green robes and an old friend of Ispin. He was on his way to Vogler to say his goodbyes.
  • He rides with the party in their caravan.
  • Roshi turns her divine sense on him and sees a mortal man with a faint golden aura.
  • He tells Mayriel that clerics have not been seen for some three hundred years. If the gods are giving people magic again, there must be a cause. She names the Platinum Dragon as her god, and he approves.
  • He warns that the Test of High Sorcery is terrifying and can be cutthroat.
  • He asks whether Wyhan in Kalaman is the one to see. Wyhan is rough around the edges and very powerful, and his advice is to be courteous.
Vogler
  • The party reaches Vogler on the Vingaard River. The village is preparing for a festival, and Ispin's celebration of life is to be held at the Brass Crab.
  • Roshi heads for the Fish Bowl. Mayriel walks down to the docks.
  • Jasper explores and learns of Thornwall Keep.
  • Alone in the market square, Jasper notices a hunched old man with a shimmer about him. Inside the old man's shape he sees a much smaller figure, and it gives him a knowing look.
