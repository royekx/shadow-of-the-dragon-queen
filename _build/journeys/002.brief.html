After the Ambush
  • The dead knights had been investigating attacks in the region. The party takes what clues they carried, and Roshi sees to the dead.
  • The scaled bodies decay with disturbing speed. The party burns them.
Dreams on the Road
  • Jasper dreams of the Dragon's Eye, the guild that bound him with a deadly coin, and of Lucky, its guildmaster. His task is to enter the world of mages, find the moving Tower of Wayreth, and steal the Orb of Dragonkind. None shall hide from the Dragon's Eye.
  • On watch, Kakashi sees the moons align in a way that feels deliberate. Voices tell him he is not strong enough, not yet. He wants greater magic, urgently.
The Man in the Sand
  • A man is half-swallowed by the earth, one foot sticking out of the ground. Jasper approaches and is pulled in too.
  • A living mass of sand rises around them. Roshi is nearly killed, and Mayriel keeps her alive.
  • The old man inside freezes the creature solid, and the party shatters it.
  • He is Fizban, a wizard in green robes and an old friend of Ispin. He takes an interest in Mayriel's divine magic, since clerics have not walked openly in centuries, and gives Kakashi counsel on wizardry.
Vogler
  • The party reaches Vogler on the Vingaard River, busy with the Kingfisher Festival and Ispin's funeral.
  • Jasper notices an unusual figure near Thornwall Keep.
  • The same rumor comes from several mouths: the fish are vanishing, and strange sounds have been heard from the river.
