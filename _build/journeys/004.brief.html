Ispin's Funeral
  • At sunset the town gathers by the river. Becklin speaks. Ispin had asked for stories at his funeral instead of tears.
  • Becklin and Cudgel push his boat into the current, and it sinks from sight.
  • At the Brass Crab the stories begin. Bakaris the Younger mocks them, and Becklin sends him off for the evening.
  • Roshi tells of Ispin and the bear den. Kakashi speaks of a man guided by curiosity rather than fear. The room goes still, then raises its drinks.
Plans and Gifts
  • Becklin says Ispin meant his green shield to pass on. Roshi accepts it.
  • Jasper finds a note in his pocket and goes alone to General Goods. Old Man Jenkins puts a blade to his throat and gives half a passphrase. Jasper does not know the rest.
  • Jasper's coin shows him a smaller figure inside the old man's disguise. Jenkins is Dragon's Eye too, and had feared that Lucky sent Jasper to kill him.
  • He teaches Jasper the phrase, warns him about Lucky and the coin, and calls the tower job a death sentence. He sells Jasper a pair of winged boots, paid for with the party's shared gold.
  • Aratos and Mayriel tell Becklin their visions. She promises a letter that may win them an audience in Kalaman.
  • In the morning Fizban leaves for Kalaman and takes Aeimos with him. Mayriel wakes to a golden finch and a pendant.
  • At Thornwall Keep, Becklin gives Roshi Ispin's Green Shield. It feels as though the shield has come home.
The Mock Battle
  • The Kingfisher Festival ends with a re-enactment at High Hill. Darrett, Becklin's squire, leads the militia. Cudgel's mercenaries play the old Istarian foe.
  • A mercenary strikes with an unpadded blade, and a villager screams.
  • Jasper kills a traitor before Darrett can fall. Aratos stays at Darrett's side. Kakashi fights from the air in the winged boots.
  • More attackers ride in, and with them a huge half-ogre. It nearly crushes Roshi. Her blade answers with divine fire.
  • Jasper shoots the half-ogre through the head, and Roshi ducks behind Ispin's shield as it falls.
  • Becklin and Cudgel drive the rest off. Cudgel is horrified that her own people turned.
  • On the half-ogre is a note: For your services from the Red Army.
