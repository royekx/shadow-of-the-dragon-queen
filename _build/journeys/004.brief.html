Ispin's Funeral
  • At sunset the town gathers by the river. Becklin speaks. Ispin had asked for tales at his funeral instead of tears.
  • Villagers carry his boat to the dock's edge. Becklin and Cudgel lower it into the river, and it drifts out and sinks from sight.
  • At the Brass Crab the stories begin: the volcano, the mountain, Becklin's wyrmling, Fizban's stolen pie.
  • Roshi tells of Ispin and the bear den.
  • Mayriel, who only knew of Ispin, speaks of a man guided by curiosity rather than fear. The room goes silent, then raises its drinks.
  • Bakaris the Younger heckles all evening. After the toast he says none of the tales are true, and Becklin sends him off.
The Shield and the Road Ahead
  • Fizban must leave for Kalaman in the morning. He says Mayriel's vision worries him and that he will try to arrange an audience there.
  • Aratos wants to warn the Knights of Solamnia. Mayriel agrees to travel with the others for a while.
  • Becklin says Ispin left his green shield to Fizban. Fizban declines it and offers it to Ispin's friends. Roshi asks for it, and Kakashi backs her.
  • Becklin asks Mayor Raven to house the party for the night.
  • Aratos tells Becklin his vision and Mayriel's. She knows of no such creatures, but she promises a letter that may win them an audience in Kalaman.
Jasper's Meeting
  • Jasper finds a note in his pocket and slips away alone to General Goods. Aratos means to follow and loses him.
  • Behind the shop Old Man Jenkins puts a blade to his throat and gives half a passphrase. Jasper does not know the rest.
  • Jasper's coin shows him a small figure inside the old man's shape. Jenkins is Dragon's Eye too, and had feared that Lucky sent Jasper to kill him.
  • He teaches Jasper the phrase: None shall hide from the Dragon's Eye.
  • He explains Lucky and the coin, and calls stealing the Orb of Dragonkind from the moving Tower of Wayreth insane. There are others like them in Kalaman.
  • He sells Jasper a pair of winged boots, paid for with the party's shared gold.
  • Aratos is waiting up past midnight. Jasper shows the boots, calls them a slick deal, and says nothing of the guild.
Morning Gifts
  • Fizban is gone by morning and has taken Aeimos with him. His note leaves Kakashi the spell Shield.
  • Mayriel wakes to a golden canary and a silver pendant. A voice speaks through the bird to her alone and hopes to help her more when they meet again.
  • The party divides its gear. Kakashi takes the winged boots. Roshi takes the Moon-Touched Sword.
  • Darrett, Becklin's squire, brings them to Thornwall Keep. Becklin gives Roshi Ispin's Green Shield. It feels as though the shield has come home.
  • The shield comes with one request: its bearer fights in the festival's mock battle, which Ispin loved.
  • Becklin says the nearest thing Vogler has to a spellcaster is Than, a tinkerer who lodges with her.
Before the Battle
  • Aratos wants to question Lord Bakaris about Kalaman. Darrett walks the party to the house, and they find only the son.
  • Bakaris the Younger says his family comes from Kalaman and has connections there. Aratos and Kakashi see that he has very little real authority.
  • Kakashi strikes his staff on the ground and learns that Lord Bakaris will judge the mock battle.
  • Roshi finds the shield can wrap her in the illusion of a boulder or a tree. Darrett spars with her, and Aratos tells him to trust himself.
The Mock Battle
  • At midday the mock battle is fought at High Hill. Darrett leads the militia. Cudgel's mercenaries play the old Istarian foe.
  • Kakashi flies over the field in the winged boots.
  • A mercenary's unpadded blade slashes into one of the militia, and the screaming starts. The traitors go for Darrett first.
  • Jasper kills a traitor before Darrett can fall. Aratos keeps Darrett close and calls for one attacker to be taken alive.
  • One mercenary is knocked senseless and taken alive.
  • More attackers ride in, and with them a huge half-ogre. It nearly crushes Roshi. Her blade answers with divine fire.
  • Jasper shoots the half-ogre through the head, and Roshi throws herself clear on Ispin's shield as it falls.
Aftermath
  • Becklin and Cudgel drive the rest off. Cudgel is horrified that her own people turned, and swears to get to the bottom of it.
  • Kakashi searches the half-ogre and finds a note: For your services from the Red Army.
  • Many of the militia are dead or badly hurt. Becklin sets Darrett to getting the wounded off the field.
  • The captured mercenary is locked in Vogler's jail for questioning. The party offers to help find out who ordered the attack.
