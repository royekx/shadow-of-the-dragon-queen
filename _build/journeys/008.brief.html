On the River
  • Kalaman is half a day downriver. Roshi offers Darrett Becklin's armor. He refuses it, believing he failed Vogler.
  • Lord Bakaris and his son suggest that more might have been done to save the town.
  • Kakashi attunes to the Boilerdrake Heart, the gem from the dragon machine. Aratos takes up the Emberwake Greataxe.
The Dead in the Water
  • Figures appear in the river. Villagers who reach for them are pulled under.
  • Each person sees someone different. Darrett sees Becklin. Jasper sees Lucky, who warns that time is running out. Mayriel sees the Dragon Queen, who promises that her sword will find her. Roshi sees Ispin, asking whether she truly protected the villagers.
  • Aratos sees two knights, Cathan Twice-Born and Lord Soth. They tell him he will have to choose soon.
  • The apparitions rise as Ashen Vigils, burning with violet flame. The party breaks them and pulls the taken back into the boats.
Kalaman
  • Mayor Raven asks the party and Darrett to speak for Vogler. Darrett puts on Becklin's armor before they go in.
  • Bakaris the Younger blocks the council door and calls the refugees fishfolk. Kakashi, Roshi and Aratos make clear that he does not speak for them.
  • Before Governor Miat and Marshal Vendri, Darrett says Vogler seeks refuge, not war. Aratos silences Lord Bakaris when he tries to twist the hearing.
  • The party offers its service to Kalaman in exchange for aid. Other villages have been attacked too.
While the Council Deliberates
  • A guard strikes down Kakashi's raven when he tries to send it back in.
  • Jasper overhears the private talk. Governor Miat holds firm control, and Marshal Vendri mentions Fizban and Aeimos aiding other villages.
  • In the courtyard, Squire Roberto shows Aratos statues of honored knights. Lord Soth's is among them.
  • One council member shimmers with illusion as he leaves. Jasper recognizes the Dragon's Eye.
The Decision
  • Governor Miat grants refuge on one condition: the party aids Kalaman's military efforts. Darrett agrees to act as intermediary so the party is not formally conscripted.
  • Aratos names himself a Knight Radiant, and Marshal Vendri acknowledges the title. Vendri asks for a name for the party.
  • At her apothecary, Wyhan reads Kakashi's and Jasper's scrolls. Both will test for the White Robes, several weeks out.
  • The golden finch, Sunscale, chooses to stay with Mayriel when Fizban speaks of leaving.
