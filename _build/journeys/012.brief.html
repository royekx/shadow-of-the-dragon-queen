Down from the Hill
  • Sergeant Boyd watches the monument fall and climbs the hill with two travelers shut in with the village: Jumanji, a tall bard with a flute, and Tien, a small tinkerer who works her magic through thimbles. They come from Barrowmere with a letter for Kalaman.
  • Introductions at the top. Aratos Stormblessed is working his jaw where Roshi dropped him. For a moment Kakashi's eyes turn red, split by a black slit.
  • The curse still holds. Three things have to be found to break it.
  • Boyd names two people: old Gribb Brack, whose farm feeds the village, and Marigold, the village grandmother and healer. Her cottage is in plain sight, but no path reaches it.
  • The party splits. Roshi, Kakashi and Jasper go to the farm. Aratos, Mayariel, Boyd and the guests go into the village.
The Black Seed
  • Gribb has lived here eighty-four years. Crops grow only under the black moon, then die. Frostlace means waiting for the black moon and fighting the wolves.
  • Jasper watches an echo of a younger Gribb bury something in an untended corner of the field. He digs and finds roots that pulse with light.
  • Kakashi reads the roots: the magic in them drains life. At their source is a jet-black knot shaped like a seed. They cut the roots and lift it whole.
  • Two black wolves rise out of the ground. Roshi wounds one and Kakashi's lightning burns it to ash. Jasper shoots the other from behind a shed.
  • The black moon locks over the white. Howling rises from the hill.
The White Tooth
  • Ked sits bandaged on the sickhouse porch, still faintly marked red and black.
  • Sela tends the sick. Mayariel notices that she alone is untouched by the white moon's sickness. Sela sets Aratos's jaw. Marigold helped everyone through last winter's fever, and no one has seen her since the village closed.
  • Sela's husband Miren lets slip that some say Marigold granted people things, then changes the subject.
  • In the workshop, Aratos asks Miren about the pull he feels from the axe. Miren's advice: find Marigold, a wizard or a cleric, and don't bottle it up.
  • Tien finds a pendant on Sela: a crescent of bone glowing with holy, healing power, most likely a hobgoblin's tusk. Miren gave it to her when she fell sick with the Pale Fever.
  • The party tells Sela the truth. She hands the pendant to Tien, and Miren begs her not to.
  • Sela withers into a dead thing and lunges at Tien. Tien's ray of frost drops her. Oh no. I didn't want to kill her.
  • Miren confesses. When everyone else recovered and Sela did not, he went to Marigold, and the pendant brought Sela back.
  • Outside, every echo turns and walks toward the hill.
The Red Banner
  • The seed belongs to black and the tooth to white. The last gift belongs to red.
  • Boyd tells the story of the Blood Summer, twenty-seven years ago. Hobgoblins besieged the village and cut it off from Kalaman, which it fed. His wife did not survive it. Becklin brought a banner and it was flown over the gate. After that the hobgoblins never came back.
  • The banner is a grain sack painted with the village's mark. Jasper takes it down, and a hobgoblin captain forms out of red wisps behind him.
  • Roshi's arrow strikes near its heart, and Jumanji finishes it with an insult. The captain's tusk is the same shape as the pendant.
  • The eclipse forms hours early, and the monument is whole again on the hill.
  • Boyd joins them: I will always lay down my life for the people I protect.
The Hill Again
  • The hill is full of wolves, red hobgoblins and echoes. The monument's slams send waves of white, red and black down the slope.
  • Mayariel raises a sphere of silver light. Boyd fights like the soldier he is: just like before.
  • A black wave takes Jasper, boots and all, and the three gifts fall where he stood. Kakashi sees the weave that moved him. His ice dims the white ring.
  • The Emberwake forces itself into Aratos's hand, and now there is pleasure in the killing. The others keep their distance.
  • The light washes over Roshi and she remembers the bear den, where she stepped in front of Ispin. Vines grow from the green shield and hold her fast. She hears Ispin's chuckle. From then on the hill cannot move her.
Tea with Marigold
  • Jasper is in a cozy cottage with Marigold, who offers him ginseng tea. He doesn't drink it.
  • Her earrings and necklace carry the monument's three rings, and the red one is lit. In the corner are clothes that would fit someone much younger. She calls her jewelry offerings to the moons.
  • When he tries to leave, the urge to sit back down is too strong. There's no need to be hostile, young Jasper. He never told her his name.
  • He shatters the red earring. She looks sad and sends him back. On the hill the red ring goes dead and the hobgoblins vanish.
Bracken Hollow Fights
  • Jumanji takes the seed. The pull seizes him and he refuses to go. Tien takes the banner.
  • The villagers charge up the hill behind Boyd, Corvin and Ked among them, with rakes and shovels and spears.
  • Aratos's javelin cracks the monument's chest. Mayariel's silver-gold bolt takes the shape of a dragon, and the black ring cracks.
  • Jasper gathers all three gifts and is taken again. Kakashi watches the weave carry him off and understands it.
  • Tien's glittering dust lights up the monument, and her ray of frost cracks the red ring. Only the white is left.
  • A second mark opens in Kakashi's eye. He turns the working on the monument and sends it away. The cost leaves him deeply drained.
  • Aratos lets the rage go. Not now. He scoops up the gifts and drops into the cavern.
Eighty-Four Years
  • The monument crashes down in Marigold's garden. Her face slips: longer, peeling, with long ears. Everything was fine until fools appeared. But I guess 84 years of feeding is good enough.
  • Jasper breaks a second charm and is flung back.
  • Mayariel seats the white tooth, Jumanji the red banner, Tien the black seed.
  • Light tears the dome apart. The real sky returns, a cloudy day, and the moons fade.
  • Marigold, in all their minds: I got what I needed. Perhaps we'll meet again, and you won't be as successful. Then she is gone.
Two Roads
  • Boyd thanks them and offers supplies. Crops grow again, and the missing animals are back. Kakashi has everything on his list.
  • A raven from Wyhan: the Test of High Sorcery is postponed because of the war, and Fizban is in the apothecary demanding the wand and the shield.
  • Roshi, Kakashi, Jumanji and Tien go west to Kalaman. Aratos, Mayariel and Jasper go on to find Tatina Rookledust.
