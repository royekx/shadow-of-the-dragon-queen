The Evacuation
  • The party gets the villagers of Vogler down to the boats.
  • Aratos, Mayariel, Jasper and Kakashi help Than clear draconians out of Thornwall Keep. Than stays behind to gather two more things.
  • Aratos and Roshi bring Becklin's chest down from the keep, as she asked.
A Dragon Over Vogler
  • The last boats are loading when wings darken the sky. A dragon breathes fire across the docks.
  • Than launches himself from the burning keep on the gnome-flinger and lands on his feet. From the air he sees smaller winged shapes in the smoke, and a rider on the dragon's back.
  • Mayariel raises a twilight glow around the party. Jasper takes to the air in the winged boots and shoots.
Theros Flametongue
  • The rider jumps down: green-skinned and orcish, with a flaming axe. He says he has heard of heroes on the front lines and cannot see any.
  • Aratos rages, the shapes of his ancestors gather around him, and he draws first blood. The orc gives his name as a courtesy to the one he means to kill: Theros Flametongue.
  • A red draconian that Than shoots convulses and explodes as it dies.
  • Theros tears the axe from Aratos's hands and knocks Mayariel flat. Aratos fights on with his sword and takes the axe back up.
  • From the air, Jasper sees that the dragon is a machine with a crew seated inside it. He tells the others.
  • The machine's fire catches Theros along with the party. He buckles for a moment.
  • Kakashi strikes Theros with lightning. Mayariel drives him to one knee, and her spiritual sword goes through his throat.
The Machine Falls
  • Aratos hurls his javelin of lightning. The elder warrior's rough old voice says one word in his ear: good. Lightning tears through the machine, and it crashes into the docks.
  • Roshi and Aratos go down in the crash. The periapt Roshi wears keeps her from death. Mayariel heals Aratos.
  • Kakashi kills the last operator in the wreck. The blast drops him too, and a memory surfaces: a village set aflame, people burning and crying.
  • Aratos throws again. Cold runs down his arm and the elder warrior's voice asks: are you worthy? The javelin changes shape for a moment, changes back, and misses.
  • A creature throwing lightning breaks Mayariel's blessing. Jasper kills it, and it bursts apart on Than.
  • Kakashi pulls a hot, glowing gem from the wreck. Aratos takes Theros's still-warm axe.
Leaving Vogler
  • Jeyev carries Darrett out of the smoke. Becklin and Cudgel ordered him to take the boy and run. Vogler is being occupied.
  • Than wakes Roshi with a potion, readies the boat, and leaves Vogler aboard it with the party.
  • Kakashi urges them to go, help the villagers who escaped, and spread word of what they saw.
  • Finbar, visible to Jasper alone, boards the boat. The others hear a voice from nowhere: glad to see you're not dead yet.
  • Mayariel offers Paladine her family signet ring and swears to do what he asks when the time comes. The ring glows and stays on her hand. The golden canary appears, and its light heals the party.
The River
  • From the water the party sees Becklin and Cudgel struggling to hold back a tall woman with charred skin, blackened armor and a massive sword. Cudgel is kicked through a building.
  • Becklin reaches the edge of the dock and raises her hands as if to say it is all right. Two slashes cross her back, and she falls into the water.
  • A figure stands over the body of Theros. Flame passes across him, and he rises.
  • The charred woman reaches toward the boat: run as far as you can. You will not escape the flames of the Dragon Queen.
