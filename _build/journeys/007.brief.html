A Dragon Over Vogler
  • The last boats are loading when wings darken the sky. A dragon breathes fire across the docks.
  • Than launches himself from the burning keep on his gnome-flinger and lands on his feet. He sees smaller winged shapes in the smoke, and a rider on the dragon's back.
  • Mayriel raises a twilight glow around the party. Jasper takes to the air in the winged boots and shoots.
Theros Flametongue
  • The rider lands: green-skinned and orcish, with a flaming axe. He names himself Theros Flametongue and goes for Aratos.
  • Aratos rages, and the shapes of his ancestors gather around him.
  • A red draconian that Than shoots convulses and explodes as it dies.
  • From the air, Jasper sees that the dragon does not move like flesh. It is a machine, crewed by figures seated inside it.
  • Kakashi strikes Theros with lightning. Mayriel drives him to one knee, and her spiritual sword goes through his throat.
The Machine Falls
  • Aratos hears the cold voice again and hurls a javelin. Lightning tears through the machine, and it crashes into the docks.
  • Roshi goes down, kept from death by the magic she carries.
  • Kakashi kills the last operator in the wreck. The blast drops him too, and a memory surfaces: a village burning, people crying.
  • Mayriel offers Paladine her family signet ring and a promise of obedience. The ring glows, the golden finch appears, and its light restores the party.
  • Kakashi pulls a hot, glowing gem from the wreck. Aratos takes Theros's still-warm axe.
The River
  • Jeyev carries Darrett from the front line to the boats. Finbar, unseen by most, makes for a boat as well.
  • From the water the party sees Becklin and Cudgel holding back a tall woman with charred skin, blackened armor and a massive sword. Cudgel is kicked through a building.
  • Becklin reaches the edge of the dock and raises her hands as if to say it is all right. Blades strike her from behind, and she falls into the water.
  • A figure stands over Theros's body. Flame passes across him, and he rises.
  • A voice calls after the boats: run as far as you can. You will not escape the flames of the Dragon Queen.
