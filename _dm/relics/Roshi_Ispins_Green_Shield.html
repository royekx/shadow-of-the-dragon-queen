The Rule of the Shield

  • It answers memory. The shield reacts to what its bearer remembers of Ispin. Someone who did not know him gets a +1 shield and nothing more.
  • The memory has to be hers. She was there. Tall tales told by others do not count, though she can go and learn what really happened.
  • She tells it, you build it. When Roshi's player tells a memory of Ispin in character, you turn it into one ability and name it after the story.
  • It speaks in memories. There is a little of Ispin in the shield, and for now it reaches her only by bringing a memory to mind. It nudges her toward protecting people and stays quiet on puzzles.
  • Keep it apart from the axe. Cathan tests and judges Aratos. The shield is warm, and it asks nothing.

Building an Ability from a Memory

  • Cost: one leaf for most. Two for something that changes a whole fight.
  • Size: one leaf buys about what a 2nd-level spell does, for a minute or for one big moment.
  • Shape: take the one impossible thing that happened in the story and let it happen again.
  • Lane: her paladin features already take hits and saves for other people. Give the shield things an oath does not do.

Slumbering

Ispin's Green Shield (Slumbering)

Shield, +1 · requires attunement by someone who remembers Ispin

Green Disguise. As played: she can wrap herself in the illusion of a boulder or a tree.

A Life Worth Living. Once per long rest, when she would drop to 0 hit points, she drops to 1 instead.

Shield Bash. After she hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, she can use a bonus action to bash it. The target makes a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + her proficiency bonus + her Strength modifier). On a failure she pushes it 5 feet or knocks it prone. The target can be up to one size larger than her.

Leaves: 2 (regained on a long rest). They show as leaves on the painted tree.

Memories: 1.

The Bear Den, 1 leaf. Bonus action, lasts 1 minute. She has advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and she cannot be moved or knocked prone against her will. Her Shield Bash works on a creature of any size, pushes it 15 feet and knocks it prone, and deals an extra 1d8 bludgeoning damage.

Oath of the Flowercrown: Notes for a Rewrite

Roshi's oath is a homebrew from 2020. Her player's time away is a good window to rewrite it. These are notes, and nothing is decided.

  • Every feature is borrowed, and none of them expresses flowers, hospitality, kindness or memory.
  • The Vengeance options fit the tenets poorly, and Vow of Enmity is among the strongest any oath has.

Why the Oath of Misfortune Is the Model

  • One resource: Jinx charges, from level 3.
  • One tag: "cursed." Every later feature keys off it.
  • Tenets that say how she fights.
  • Its one hot spot: Jinx a Creature costs no action, allows no save, and can name an ability used for saving throws. If it ever needs trimming, limit it to attack rolls and ability checks, or add a Charisma save.

A Direction

Make it the mirror of Misfortune. Misfortune curses enemies. Flowercrown blesses the people under her care.

  • Resource: flowers in the crown, a few per long rest.
  • Tag: "guest." Giving someone a flower puts them under her hospitality.
  • What features key off: her protection reaches guests further and works better on them.
  • Level 7: keep Self-Sacrifice. It already fits.
  • Channel Divinity: one option about shelter and one about courage, in place of the two from Vengeance.
  • Spell list: drop crown of madness.
  • Wording: Misfortune is written in 2024 terms and the table runs 2014 classes. Write this one in 2014 terms unless the conversion starts here.

How the oath and the shield divide the work. The oath decides who she protects. The shield is how his memory helps her do it.
