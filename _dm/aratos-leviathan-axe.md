## What the player and the PC know

- The name "Leviathan Axe". The player is a God of War fan and the axe was built for him, so the name is fine to use on the site. The quest is titled The Leviathan Axe.
- The tree of fire and frost, and the choice: blades of fury or an axe of cold restraint (Session 5).
- Cathan Twice-Born is the one testing him: the figure at the tree and the voice (see above).
- Aratos chose the cold side at the reliquary. The Emberwake briefly became the weapon from his vision, then turned back (Session 10).
- The King has spoken three times, all in Session 10: "The same blood flows through my veins. The same weakness. Don't let it take you." at the campfire, "Don't let it overtake you." in Kalaman, and "You must test your resolve." at the reliquary.
- Cathan Twice-Born is his ancestor, whose failure brought ruin (Session 10 campfire). He saw Cathan in the river (Session 8). Sir Cthondor named him and Aeimos children of Cathan (Session 9).
- The Emberwake is cursed. He cannot hand it away or set it down. Other weapons feel wrong in his hand.
- The three spirits appear when he rages. Their looks were described in Session 9.

## The mechanic

- The Emberwake is a cursed Berserker Greataxe.
- **Trigger:** Aratos takes damage while raging. Wisdom save.
- **Fail:** berserk for 1 minute. He attacks the nearest creature, friend or foe. The axe runs hot. Progress stays where it was.
- **Success:** the axe goes briefly, visibly cold. Mark one.
- **Third success:** the Emberwake becomes the Leviathan Axe (Slumbering). The hooded spirit nods.
- Failures do not erase successes. Progress carries across fights.
- Pressure valve: if he ends his rage before the triggering hit, he does not go berserk. It never counts as a success.
- Using any weapon other than the Emberwake: disadvantage (ruled at the table in Session 11).

**DC discrepancy to settle:** the full design doc says a flat DC 15. The July design chat used an escalating 14, then 16, then 18.

| Trial | Earns | Min level | Pressure | The test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| King | Slumbering (+1, 1d4 cold, Embed and Recall, King's Perimeter, 2 frost charges) | 4 | Internal | Three successful saves, while choosing to protect others over taking the kill |
| Storm | Stirring (+2, 1d6 cold, Storm's Intercession, 4 charges) | 6 | Survival | Hold a chokepoint alone so a caster can finish. Stand fast, and leave the killing blow on the leader to someone else. |
| Blade | Awakened (+3, 1d8 cold, Ghost's Discipline, 6 charges) | 8 | Provocation | An enemy who taunts him toward the fire. The killing blow must be cold. |

Storm and Blade are loose sketches. The plan is to rework them organically as the campaign goes, not run them as written.
