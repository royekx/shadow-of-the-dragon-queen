The Truth

Marigold, the healer in the cottage past the edge, is one face of something much older. Every 27 years she gave the village a gift made from what the last trouble left behind, and asked that it be kept quiet. A month ago, on the Night of the Eye, all three came due.

Tonight: learn the three gifts, take them back, and seat them in the nook at the top of the hill.

Scene 4 The Cottage Run One Visit at a Time
Read Aloud — Taken

The light closes over you, and then it is a kitchen. A fire, a pot, bundles of herbs drying from the beams, and along one wall a long row of small things: a child's shoe, a pipe, a wedding band. An old woman looks up from the pot as if you had knocked. She wears a pendant and two earrings, each cut with a mark you have seen on the Stone's chest, and one of them is glowing.

The turn
Each taken player waits here until their own next turn, takes it, and is then sent back to where they stood, with no save. Two or three can be here at once.
They can
Break the glowing talisman. AC 13, one hit. It is the color that took them, and only the lit one can be harmed. That gift can never be taken again. All three broken destroys her, and the hill goes on.
Look. One more detail of the row on the wall. Leave what she wants them for unsaid.
Talk. She plays the kind old healer, pleased to have company, and gives nothing away.
Swing at her. It lands like a blow on a sack of straw. She tuts. The talismans are what hold her together.
Marigold
An ordinary old woman who has aged alongside Gribb. This one is a puppet, with something far away holding the strings. She never attacks. The light was built to bring her one frightened villager at a time, so armed strangers in her kitchen are a problem: she puts them back on the hill and lets the Stone deal with them.
Her exit
The puppet comes apart when the third talisman breaks or when the spell does, whichever comes first. As it goes she says, in essence: that is enough, she got more out of this than she expected, and they are interesting. She will be keeping an eye on them. If the spell breaks with nobody in the kitchen, the party finds her in the doorway, already coming apart. The hearth is cold and has been for weeks. The fire they saw was never there.
Roshi · The Shield Wakes landed

What it is. The shield answers her memories of Ispin. Each one she tells can become an ability.

Trigger. The first time a light wave falls on her, or on anyone within 5 feet of her, the story she told at his funeral comes to mind: the bear den, the honey, the bears waking, and her stepping in front of him. Then ask what she does.

If she stands in the light, for herself or for someone else, it breaks on the shield and nobody is taken by that wave. Boyd sees it. The bear den wakes until the spell breaks.

The Bear Den. Advantage on Strength checks and saves, and she cannot be moved. After she hits with a melee attack, a bonus action bashes: any size, pushed 15 feet, prone, +1d8.

On the Stone. A bash knocks it flat, and it loses its next Slam.

If she is taken first. Marigold looks at the shield and says someone is in there.

After. Slumbering: +1, the boulder-or-tree disguise, A Life Worth Living (drop to 1 instead of 0, once a day), Shield Bash, two leaves, one memory.

Kakashi · The Second Mark landed

Wakes. The first time someone is taken in front of him, a second mark turns in his eye. It fades once he has cast the copy, or when the eclipse ends.

The light is Banishment (4th level, Charisma save).

Copy. Reaction when he sees a light wave. Arcana DC 19, with advantage under the eclipse. Fail: 1d4 psychic (reduced for tonight). He may try every round.

On a success. One cast within the minute, no slot. Two points of exhaustion. Sealed until wizard level 7.

His copy follows her rule: the target is gone for one round. On the Stone, that is a round with no attack and no Slams, and it lands in Marigold's garden, outside her window.

Defense. If he carries, Perception DC 14 gives advantage on the save.

If he asks to cast it again. Allowed under the eclipse, with no new check. Each cast adds two more exhaustion: four, then six, and six is death. Tell him that before he chooses. If he dies, see the Closing.
