Previous Session

The Embers of Vogler arrived at the walls of Kalaman as evening settled. The council granted refuge to the Vogler survivors, Darrett donned Becklin's armor, and the party went hunting — only to discover the hidden reliquary of the Knights Radiant beneath the hill, face a necromancer serving Lord Soth, and free a corrupted knightly spirit. They came back with a bear. The stew was good.

Session 10 opens at the fire. The evening is winding down. There is a moment of relative peace before the war asks something of them again.

Main Plot This Session

Evening — Three things are in play simultaneously: Fizban's recipe handout hides a puzzle for anyone curious enough to look; Jasper slips away when the moment is right for his Dragon's Eye meeting at Pellar's Provisions; and the rest of the party has open time for RP, camp life, and the character vignettes to find their moment naturally. Morning / Day — The city opens up. Truly free time in Kalaman — map in hand, no agenda until midday. Around midday Darrett finds them in Becklin's armor with word from Marshal Vendri: the First Mission. They are sent to find inventor Tatina Rookledust. Before departing they can speak with Than Rookledust — somewhere in the camp — who gives them real intel on how to approach her. The party leaves when they are ready but within the day. Session ends at departure or shortly into travel.

Strong Start
Read Aloud

Open at the fire. The stew is done, the people of Vogler are eating around it, and Fizban is holding court somewhere in the middle of a story that has at least two things wrong with it. Before the evening winds down, he produces a crumpled, stained piece of paper — Ispin's stew recipe, recorded by Fizban the Fabulous, for posterity. He presents it with ceremony and mild self-satisfaction. Give the handout to the players.

Character Vignettes

Short end-of-night beats — 1 to 3 minutes each unless the player leans in. Run them when the trigger presents itself naturally. Do not force sequencing on Kakashi and Roshi.

Jasper Seredyn's Territory Evening — When He Slips Away
Trigger & Setup

Finbar finds Jasper himself — no note, no signal. He simply appears at Jasper's elbow during the camp's evening noise, solid and unhurried in his Pellar disguise, and says something brief: "Walk with me." He doesn't ask twice. He leads Jasper through a quarter of the city to Pellar's Provisions. Through a hatch behind the supply shelf, down stone steps, into Fin's Bar — a vaulted cellar that predates the building above it, amber candlelight, a well-kept bar, twenty people who don't look at each other, southern wines that haven't been on the open market since the Dragon Army crossed the border. Seredyn Valcoran is at the best table. Not a corner — the center. Finbar sets drinks, takes a seat to the side, and doesn't speak again unless addressed.

Read Aloud — Seredyn's Opening

Finbar says your name once — less an introduction than a handoff — and steps back. Seredyn looks at you for a long moment. "Palanthas may have placed the coin in your hand. But Kalaman decides which doors open while you are here." A pause. "You are not mine. That does not mean you are free."

What Seredyn Covers
  • The Dragon's Eye has assets inside Kalaman's military and council — coin-marked and unmarked both.
  • Lucky's timeline is being compressed by the war. The Wayreth window is not permanent.
  • The Test of High Sorcery is Jasper's fastest path to the Tower. Wyham's sponsorship puts him closer than Lucky expected.
  • The guild is watching the full party. That observation has a shape — Seredyn doesn't elaborate.
  • Jasper's task: Watch for unsanctioned arcane activity in Kalaman. Report to Finbar. Nothing more is asked tonight.
Subtext — The Room

Finbar built this bar. Seredyn uses it as if the question of ownership is settled. It isn't. Finbar's stillness is patience, not deference. DC 14 Insight: Seredyn sitting in the center of this room is a considered mockery, and Finbar is absorbing it the way a man absorbs something he intends to answer later. The mark pulse is Lucky's alone — Seredyn has no such reach over a coin-marked operative, whatever he implies.

Recognition phrase: "The eye closes." / "Only to remember."

Aftereffect: On the way out, the bartender — one of Finbar's people — slides Jasper a second drink without being asked. That's Finbar. The first small thing returned.

Aratos Cathan at Yggdrasil That Evening — Campfire Trance

Trigger: Aratos staring into the campfire — a waking trance, eyes open, still present to anyone watching.

The campfire becomes Yggdrasil — still small, still burning, roots curling where the coals were, branches where the flame reaches. At the base of the tree, a black rose pushes slowly up through the ash, thorns catching the light. On the far side of the fire — there for only a moment, hooded, watching — stands a figure Aratos doesn't recognize. A king who doesn't look like one. He holds Aratos's gaze long enough to make the silence mean something. Then, quietly — not to Aratos, almost to himself:

Aragorn

"The same blood flows in my veins. The same weakness."

Then he's gone. The fire is just a fire again.

Purpose: Seeds the Trial of the King without naming it. Aragorn isn't warning Aratos — he's speaking about himself, and Aratos hears it anyway. The black rose sits unresolved. The Emberwake is warm.

Mayariel Kansaldi Dream That Night — Sleeping

Trigger: Mayariel sleeps — same night, after the fire dies down.

She "awakens" on cold pale stone beneath falling ash. Kansaldi stands over her wreathed in red flame and drives a heated blade toward her chest. Key images: smoke-choked sky, ash like snow, Kansaldi's burning eye, a blade glowing forge-hot, Mayariel's signet flaring platinum — the blade stopping just short of her heart. Behind Kansaldi: a broken pale road leading toward five shadowed dragon shapes around a crown.

Quest Phrase

"Rise before the sword falls."

Aftereffect: Mayariel wakes with the signet warm and the phantom pressure of the blade near her heart.

Purpose: Begins the signet quest. Frames Kansaldi as the sword sent to extinguish Paladine's chosen.

Roshi The Shield Has Grown Floating — Villager Interaction

Trigger: A Vogler villager — someone who knew Ispin well, an older woman, a former neighbor, a fisherman's wife — spots the shield in the firelight and stops. She doesn't say much. Maybe just "That's Ispin's", or she reaches out and doesn't quite touch it. A moment of shared grief. When the villager moves on, Roshi looks down at the shield properly.

The painted tree has changed subtly. A small branch now curls from the trunk near an old scar. A few tiny leaves are visible. No glow, no voice, no pulse — and the new paint looks old, as if it has always been there.

Aftereffect: When Roshi touches the new leaves, she feels like the shield has remembered something.

Purpose: The villager grounds the grief before the supernatural hint. The shield change reads as a response — as if being seen again did something.

Kakashi Previous Watcher Reflection Floating — Pull Naturally

Trigger: Activated Sharingan reflected in dark water, polished metal, glass, or the empty stew pot — any quiet solo moment.

His reflection is briefly replaced by a female non-human Watcher — ash-marked skin, long ears or subtle hornlike ridges, eyes like his but older and exhausted. A smoke-filled tower. Shelves of sealed relics shaking. A lunar-carved stone door. Blood on her hand. Something wrapped in cloth and starlight, held close.

The Watcher

"Do not watch to know. Watch to preserve."

Aftereffect: His eyes ache, as if they have been open for centuries.

Purpose: Confirms he is replacing a prior Watcher without explaining the full history.

Scene 1The RecipeEvening · Puzzle
Purpose
Plant a puzzle players can engage with at their own pace. Reward curiosity without demanding it.
Location
Vogler refugee camp — around the fire

The evening is winding down. Fizban produces a crumpled, stained piece of paper with ceremony — Ispin's stew recipe, recorded by Fizban the Fabulous, for posterity. He presents it like it cost him something. It did not. Give the handout to the players.

DM Note

The recipe is a disguised spell formula for Green-Flame Blade. The first letter of each ingredient in order spells GREEN FLAME BLADE. The preparation text reinforces it and Fizban's closing note — "fire first, blade second, green herbs third" — is the casting order. Most useful to Kakashi but worth checking against other builds. If players ask Fizban directly: "It's a recipe. Very good with root vegetables." Leave it open regardless — the handout is theirs and can be cracked any session.

Possible Outcomes
Players solve it → transcribe the cantrip
Players notice something's off but don't crack it → lingers in their hands
Ignored entirely → resurfaces any time; shelf life is the whole campaign
Reward: Green-Flame Blade (cantrip)

Scene 4The SummonsMidday · Transition
Purpose
Transition into the mission arc. Darrett in Becklin's armor is doing narrative work before a word is spoken. First official use of the party name.
Location
Wherever the party has drifted to by midday

Darrett finds them. He has been looking for a little while — methodically, not frantically. When he arrives he doesn't announce himself immediately. He just arrives, and the armor arrives with him. He's not comfortable in it yet — he carries it like something borrowed rather than earned, which is exactly what it is and exactly what he's trying to change.

Marshal Vendri has an assignment. He was told to bring them in. That's all he says before he waits to see if they follow.

DM Note — The Armor

Don't rush past this beat. Becklin is gone, Darrett is trying to become something in her absence, and the party knows both things. Let the image land before anyone speaks. If any party member has unresolved feeling about Becklin's death — Aratos in particular — this is a natural moment. Don't force it but don't close the door.

DM Note — The Name

When the party arrives at Castle Kalaman, Vendri asks — practically, without ceremony — what name she should record for the group. Kalaman's military keeps records. She needs something to put down. Let the players give it. If they give it, she writes it without comment and moves on. A name in a military ledger in a city at war means something different than a name said around a campfire.

Scene 5Vendri's Briefing & the Than ConnectionMidday · Mission Launch
Purpose
Launch the First Mission. Make it personal through Than. The Embers leave Kalaman with a name, a mission, and a thread they either picked up or were handed.
Location
Castle Kalaman — Vendri's office or war room. Stone walls, maps on the table, the sound of the garrison outside.
Vendri's Briefing

She doesn't look up immediately when you enter. There are papers on the table and she is finishing something. Then she sets the pen down and looks at you properly. "Tatina Rookledust. Gnomish inventor, reliable until recently. She has something we need and she has stopped answering. I need someone outside the uniform to go find out why." She slides a folded document across the table — a rough map and a description of the workshop's last known location. "The road is not safe. It hasn't been safe for two weeks. That is why I'm asking you and not sending a patrol." A pause. "Any questions before you go?"

The Than Connection

The name Rookledust is the thread. How it surfaces depends on Scene 3:

  • If they already made the connection — one of them mentions Than before Vendri finishes. She notes it with mild interest. She didn't know he was in the camp.
  • If they don't know — Darrett falls in beside them quietly as they leave: "There's a gnome from Vogler with that name. Saw him near the east gate this morning." He doesn't push. Either way it's the party's choice whether they go to Than before they leave.
Than — If They Go
  • She doesn't trust soldiers or anyone sent by authority. Come as people, not envoys.
  • When she's deep in a project she won't surface for pleasantries — make her problem your problem.
  • Workshop security is eccentric gnomish methodology, probably stranger than traps.
  • Their split was a question of who the work belonged to. Neither one ever answered it cleanly.

Than's manner throughout: pride, bitterness, and reluctant respect he won't say plainly. He loved her work even when he couldn't stand being in the same room with her.

Departure Window

The party leaves when they are ready but within the day. Vendri doesn't say it like an order — just a fact. They have until the city gates begin their evening close to resupply, follow up on Than, and settle any unfinished business. If they haven't moved by then, Darrett finds them again. Quietly. Just standing nearby, ready to walk when they are.

Party skips Than → they depart without the intel; Tatina's reception of a group sent by authority will be cold

Closing Image

The city falls behind them. The road runs through country that looks like the war has already visited — an abandoned cart tilted into a ditch, a farmhouse with shutters closed in the wrong season, smoke on the horizon the wrong color for a hearth fire. Somewhere ahead, an inventor who stopped answering hasn't stopped working. Probably. The Embers of Vogler walk into the afternoon and the road keeps going.

Optional Road Texture
  • A refugee family moving toward Kalaman — they have news from the road ahead. Nothing specific, just the shape of danger. A village that felt wrong. Sounds at night.
  • A Kalaman patrol heading back in, tighter-lipped than they should be about what they saw. They wave the Embers through without stopping.
  • A signpost at a crossroads with one direction scratched out recently. The wood is still pale where the lettering was removed.

None of these require resolution. They are texture. The session ends while the party is still moving.

Secrets & Clues
The numbers in Fizban's recipe are misdirection. The first letters of each ingredient spell GREEN FLAME BLADE. The preparation text describes the spell. The closing note is the casting order.
Seredyn Valcoran is the shimmer Jasper clocked leaving the council chamber. He did not summon Jasper — Jasper simply recognized a fellow marked member. The Dragon's Eye's Kalaman reach is operational and deep. Seredyn holds authority here. Finbar held it before him.
Than Rookledust's last name is not a coincidence. He is Tatina's ex-husband. He knows more about how to reach her than anyone in the city. He will not volunteer it unless the name comes up first.
Darrett is still carrying Becklin's absence. He is trying to become what she needed him to be. He doesn't know yet if he's there — and the armor isn't telling him.
The Dragon Army's advance has not stopped. Kalaman has walls and a timeline. Every delay is a cost neither Vendri nor the city can afford to name openly.
The black rose in Aratos's vision is the Dragon's Eye symbol. He doesn't know this yet. It sprouted from the base of Yggdrasil without explanation. The hooded king's line does not explain it — that's the point.
