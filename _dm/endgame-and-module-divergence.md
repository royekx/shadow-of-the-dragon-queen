# Endgame and Module Divergence (DM Only)

**Nothing here is player-known unless it is listed under "Already seen at the table". None of it goes on the companion site, in a recap, or in a session doc until it happens in play.**

Last updated: 2026-10-07, current through Session 11. Captured from a planning chat during Session 12 prep.

Status tags: **Decided** = Korey's call. **Agreed** = proposed in chat and accepted. **Proposed** = suggested, awaiting a yes or no. **Open** = undecided.

---

## 1. The ending in brief

The campaign ends at the module's final battle outside Kalaman. Mayariel, Paladine's champion, faces Kansaldi Fire-Eyes, Takhisis's champion. Mayariel calls on her god and Fizban answers. He is revealed as Paladine, becomes the Platinum Dragon, and holds off a partial manifestation of Takhisis in the sky. He frees Sunscale to fight as a gold dragon, and Mayariel rides him against Kansaldi and her red dragon. The other party members each get a matched fight of their own. Afterward Sunscale leaves to bring the good dragons into the war.

- **Decided:** this is the ending Korey sees, and it lands at the end of this module.
- **Decided:** it depends on who is still at the table. Nothing is forced.

## 3. Threads that feed the ending

### Mayariel and the dragonlance

- **Decided:** the dragonlance becomes Mayariel's quest as a cleric of Paladine. This answers the open question of what Paladine wants from her.
- **Agreed:** the framing. Paladine sent the knight Sarlamir to make peace with the dragons, and Sarlamir killed the gold dragon Karavarix with the lance. Mayariel's task is to carry the lance he disgraced and finish what he would not.
- **Open:** whether she wields the lance herself, carries it for someone else to strike with, or uses it as a holy focus. Depends on her player.

### Sunscale

- **Decided:** Sunscale is one of the seven gold dragons hidden as canaries around Fizban.
- **Decided:** he is kin to Karavarix.
- **Decided:** the party does not learn they have a gold dragon with them. Hints only, until the final battle.
- **Proposed:** he is Karavarix's clutch-mate, the one who argued for restraint at Onyari and lost.
- **Proposed:** he stays a canary because all seven are bound by the dragons' oath over the stolen eggs.
- **Proposed hints, one per chapter:**
  - Chapter 4, Sarlamir's tomb: he will not go near the spearhead and sits silent on the carved stone dragon until the party leaves.
  - Chapter 5, the Wastes: rust flakes off the spearhead when Mayariel protects someone, and he is the one watching it.
  - Chapter 5, Camp Carrionclay: he will not leave the bronze dragon egg.
  - Chapter 6, Temple of Paladine: he lands on the lance for the first time as it is restored.
  - Chapter 7: the undead Karavarix sees the canary, stops, and says a name nobody at the table knows.

### Fizban, Paladine and the sealed aspect

- **Decided:** Fizban was separated from his dragon aspect. Takhisis sealed the dragon itself, the mortal dragon, at some point in or after the last war.
- **Decided:** the dragon is trapped in the Temple of Paladine and has been reaching out for help.
- **Open:** whether Fizban cannot remember this, does not fully remember it, or whether the two have always been separate and need to become one.
- **Decided:** the dragon must be released before the ending, because Fizban rejoining with it is how he becomes the Platinum Dragon.
- **Leaning:** Mayariel helps release it without knowing what she is doing. Restoring the dragonlance at the temple's altar could be one of the things needed to restore the aspect.
- **Proposed:** the voice that speaks at the altar in Chapter 6 is the sealed aspect.

### The Fonains crossover one-shot

- **Decided:** a one-shot that connects directly to Korey's other campaign, **Fonains**.
- The Fonains party has a silver pseudodragon familiar named **Eldree**. An older dragon has been speaking through Eldree. That dragon is the sealed aspect of Paladine.
- The aspect speaks through Eldree and the Fonains party is carried to Krynn, probably by Fizban, with some divine arrangement between the two pantheons.
- **Decided:** the Fonains party is the one that actually releases the dragon, either before or after Mayariel's group does its part at the temple.
- **Decided:** at some point Fizban tells the Embers he has to go and take care of something. That is him going to meet and guide the Fonains party. He then reconnects with the aspect.
- Needs real-world timing, because the Fonains campaign is still running.

**The Fonains pantheon and why it gets involved** (noted, not worked out):

- The gods of Fonains include Asmodeus, Eldath and Primus.
- Korey also added **Ryujin**, a deity from Japanese tradition, as that world's god of dragons and snakes.
- **Leaning:** Ryujin could be related to Paladine and Takhisis. That is how the Fonains gods know something is happening on Krynn.
- **Leaning:** a bargain is struck between the two pantheons for help. The Fonains gods know their party, and a champion among them, is able and willing to do what is needed.

**The timing problem** (Open):

- In Fonains, most of the dragons left a long time ago: at least a century, maybe more.
- If Sunscale's call at the end of this module is what brings the dragons, that does not line up. The Fonains party would be on Krynn at the same time as the Embers, long after their own dragons had gone.
- **Korey's option:** time travel, which the Fonains campaign has already used. The Fonains gods send that party back in time in their own world, and the crossover happens from there.
- **Proposed alternative, with no time travel:** split the dragons into two groups. The dragons of other worlds left on their own a century or more ago, because they felt Takhisis stirring, and they are still on their way. Sunscale's errand is only Krynn's own dragons, the ones bound by the oath over the eggs. The off-world dragons arriving is then a later event.
- Does not need deciding now.

### The pact and the eggs

- Module canon: the metallic dragons stay out of the war because the Dragon Armies hold their eggs. The eggs are secretly being corrupted into draconians. The module never resolves this.
- **Decided:** in this campaign the dragons return. That requires the pact to be known as broken.
- **Open:** how it becomes known, and how Fizban learns it. Leaning toward a one-shot, possibly built on the dragon egg idea.
- **Proposed:** at the final battle Fizban tells Sunscale the truth about the eggs, and that is the permission.
- Module hook available: the bronze dragon egg at Camp Carrionclay and the hidden bronze dragon Nezrah in Heart's Hollow (Chapter 5).

- **Decided:** Mayariel is matched with Kansaldi. Paladine's feud with Takhisis is personal, which is why Mayariel is at the center of the ending.
- **Decided:** Aratos is matched with Theros Flametongue. Already seeded.
- **Decided:** the champions get woven into the module as it goes.

## 6. Open questions

1. How does the pact become known as broken, and who tells Fizban?
2. Does Fizban not remember the separation, or were he and the dragon always two?
3. Timing of the crossover inside the module. The module destroys the City of Lost Names at the end of Chapter 6, when the citadel breaks free. The release has to happen before that, or in the ruins afterward.
4. Timing of the crossover across worlds. Fonains's dragons left at least a century ago. Time travel, or two groups of dragons? See the Fonains section.
5. What exactly does Mayariel do at the temple that helps release the aspect, beyond restoring the lance?
6. The matched champions for Kakashi, Jasper and Roshi. To port from ChatGPT.
7. Does Mayariel wield the lance, carry it, or channel through it?
8. What is Sunscale to Karavarix exactly: clutch-mate, or something else?
9. What form does the part of Takhisis take, and how does it get through? The citadel's dark altar is one option.
10. What does Fizban's revitalization give the party during the Time Stop?
11. How is Ryujin related to Paladine and Takhisis, and what is the bargain between the two pantheons?

## 7. If the campaign continues past the module

Korey is willing to leave canon behind at that point, so it becomes the players' own story. Ideas noted, none decided:

- The council: winning over the free peoples one faction at a time.
- A key item held by each of the five highlords.
- A heist to recover the hostage eggs, or to prove what is being done to them.
- A ritual at the temple in Neraka to bring Takhisis through in full.
- The off-world dragons arriving.
