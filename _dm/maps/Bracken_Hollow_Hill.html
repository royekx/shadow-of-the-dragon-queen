The Hill at Bracken Hollow

Numbered dots mark every 30 feet of path. The path is 150 feet long. Reach is measured from the hole, and the line itself counts as inside.

Getting Up

  • The path: normal movement, and they can fight as they go. Dashing every round, a carrier reaches the top and seats in round three.
  • Climbing straight up: an action and an Athletics check, DC 13, for 30 feet of slope. Nothing else that turn.
  • Flying: allowed. Every wave is a sphere and reaches the air.
  • The crowd is on the path and the slope. The foot and the air are clear of it.
  • A dasher ends round one at dot 2, just inside red, and round two at dot 4, just inside white.
