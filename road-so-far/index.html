Journey 011: The Moons Over Bracken Hollow

A list from Wyhan led the party into Bracken Hollow, shut for a month under three eclipsed moons. Boyd explained the turn of the moons and Corvin the part Boyd left out. On the hill the monument fell once its three rings were darkened. Beneath it is a cavern with three recesses.

The Emberwake's fury took Aratos in the middle of the fight, and Roshi had to strike him senseless. The monument marked him black before it fell. He lies on the hill, guarded by the horn's spectral warriors. Ked lies senseless nearby. Corvin has run.
