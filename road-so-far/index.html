Journey 010 — The Weight of the Axe

A recipe hid a spell, and all five now carry the Green Flame Blade. Jasper met the Dragon's Eye in Kalaman and was pointed at Wyhan. The Emberwake proved cursed. Marshal Vendri wrote the party down as the Embers and sent them to find a tinkerer.

Aratos's trophy axe is cursed. It took him in a sparring match and nearly killed a squire, and he cannot give it away. At the reliquary he held it once by choosing stillness over fury. He will need his companions when he cannot.
