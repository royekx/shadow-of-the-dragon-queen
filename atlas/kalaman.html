Kalaman
Walled city

Kalaman is a walled city a few days downriver from Vogler. It took in Vogler's refugees in exchange for the party's service. It holds a High Sorcery presence, a hidden branch of the Dragon's Eye, and a courtyard of knightly statues that includes Lord Soth.

  • Wyhan's ApothecaryComponents, potions at steep prices, and High Sorcery's business.
