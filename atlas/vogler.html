Vogler
Riverside town

Vogler was a village on the Vingaard River that knew how to mourn and celebrate in the same breath. It honored Ispin Greenshield, held the Kingfisher Festival, and was burned by the Red Dragon Army's mechanical dragon. Its people are now refugees outside Kalaman.
