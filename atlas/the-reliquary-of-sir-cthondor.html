The Reliquary of Sir Cthondor
Hidden tomb

A cave outside Kalaman opens onto a stone door marked with the sword of the Knights Radiant, drawn with six points. The door asks for an oath. Behind it lie murals of a floating city falling, and a tomb of knights with Sir Cthondor at its centre.

Within The Reliquary of Sir Cthondor
  • The oath doorLit Valor for Aratos, Resolve for Aeimos, Foresight for Roshi.
  • The mural passageKnights of Solamnia and Knights Radiant beneath a floating city, then the city falling. Defaced with purplish-black ash.
  • The tombArmored dead along the walls and the statue of Sir Cthondor with his horn.
