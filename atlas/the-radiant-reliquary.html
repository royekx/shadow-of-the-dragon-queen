The Radiant Reliquary
Tomb of fallen knights

A cave outside Kalaman opens onto a stone door marked with the sword of the Knights Radiant, drawn with six points. The door asks for an oath. Behind it, murals show Knights of Solamnia and Knights Radiant fighting together against a floating city, and standing in smaller numbers as it fell. The reliquary is the tomb of the knights who fell in that battle. Their armored dead stand around the walls of a round chamber, and Sir Cthondor stands at its center with a horn in the crook of his arm.

Within The Radiant Reliquary
  • The oath doorLit Valor for Aratos, Resolve and a third spoke for Aeimos, Foresight for Kakashi.
  • The mural passageKnights of Solamnia and Knights Radiant fighting together against a floating city, then the city falling. The horn-bearer's image is smeared with purplish-black ash.
  • The tombA round chamber for the knights who fell in the battle on the murals. Their armored dead stand along the walls. At the center is the statue of Sir Cthondor, whose horn Aratos now carries.
