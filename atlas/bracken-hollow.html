Bracken Hollow
Forest village

A plain village tucked into the forest, of the same sort as Vogler, with no wall and no guard at its gate. For about a month no one has been able to leave it: a person who walks out past its edge walks back in on the far side. The three moons hang over it in eclipse and take turns. Under the white moon people feel a little sick and echoes of the villagers walk the streets. Under the red a battle is fought in the town. Under the black the wolves come, and the farms give food. Then comes the eclipse itself, when the monument on the hill wakes and one of the black-touched vanishes. About two hundred people lived here. Around forty are gone.

Within Bracken Hollow
  • The graveyardA short walk from the square. Many of its graves are fresh.
  • Boyd's shelterA dim, run-down house where Boyd keeps villagers through the dangerous hours.
  • The farmsThey give food only under the black moon, when the wolves are out.
  • The hill and its monumentA tall block of stone that wakes and stands during the eclipse. Three rings on its chest: white, red, black.
  • The cavern beneath the hillReached by a crevice where the monument stood. Closed, with three recesses. The spell is bound here.
  • Marigold's cottageJust outside the village. Home of the healer, who was away the night it closed.
