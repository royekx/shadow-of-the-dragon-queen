Bracken Hollow
Forest village

A plain village tucked into the forest, of the same sort as Vogler, with no guard at its gate. Its crops were once the best in the region, and it was Kalaman's main source of food. For about a month no one could leave it. The three moons hung over it in eclipse and took turns, white, red and black, and at each eclipse the monument on the hill woke and one of the black-touched vanished. About forty of its two hundred people were gone. The spell was bound to three things that came to the village in hard times: a black seed buried in Gribb Brack's field, the red banner Becklin brought in the Blood Summer, and a white tooth the healer Marigold gave to save Sela from the Pale Fever. When the Embers seated all three beneath the hill, the spell broke. The true sky came back, the crops grew again, and the missing animals returned. Marigold vanished.

Within Bracken Hollow
  • The graveyardA short walk from the square. Some of its graves are fresh.
  • Boyd's shelterA dim, run-down house where Boyd keeps villagers through the dangerous hours.
  • Gribb Brack's farmThe farm that feeds the village. The black seed lay under an untended corner of its field.
  • Sela's sickhouseBuilt by the carpenter Miren, where his wife Sela tended the sick on cots until the white tooth was taken from her.
  • The gateWhere the red banner flew for twenty-seven years, since the Blood Summer.
  • The hill and its monumentA tall block of stone that woke and stood during the eclipse, with three rings on its chest: white, red, black. It fell for good when the spell broke.
  • The cavern beneath the hillReached by a crevice at the top of the hill. Three seats in its walls now hold the seed, the banner and the tooth.
  • Marigold's cottageJust outside the village, in plain sight and out of reach while the spell held. Jasper was taken there and drank none of her tea. Marigold is gone from it.
