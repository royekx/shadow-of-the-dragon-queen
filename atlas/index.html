KalamanWalled city · On the Vingaard Base of operations

Kalaman is a walled city a few days downriver from Vogler. It took in Vogler's refugees in exchange for the party's service. It holds a High Sorcery presence, a hidden branch of the Dragon's Eye, and a courtyard of knightly statues that includes Lord Soth.

The town on the Vingaard that the party could not save.

VoglerRiverside town · On the Vingaard Destroyed

Vogler was a village on the Vingaard River that knew how to mourn and celebrate in the same breath. It honored Ispin Greenshield, held the Kingfisher Festival, and was burned by the Red Dragon Army's mechanical dragon. Its people are now refugees outside Kalaman.

Thornwall KeepKeep · Vogler Lost with Vogler

The keep at Vogler. Becklin gave Roshi Ispin's shield here, Than kept his workshop here, and Becklin left a chest here for Darrett, with plate armor in it. It burned with the town.

Crustdurn IsleRiver island · Vingaard River, near Vogler Cleared

A lonely island in the river near Vogler, rumored haunted and avoided by locals. A hatch in its abandoned shack leads down to a cavern littered with fish bones.

The roads between, and places known only by name.

Bracken HollowForest village · Off the road out of Kalaman Sealed

A plain village tucked into the forest, of the same sort as Vogler, with no guard at its gate. For about a month no one has been able to leave it: a person who walks out past its edge walks back in on the far side. The three moons hang over it in eclipse and take turns. Under the white moon people feel a little sick and echoes of the villagers walk the streets. Under the red a battle is fought in the town. Under the black the wolves come, and the farms give food. Then comes the eclipse itself, when the monument on the hill wakes and one of the black-touched vanishes. A few travelers are shut in with the villagers. About two hundred people lived here. Around forty are gone.
